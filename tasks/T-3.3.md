## Specification
- **Palette:** a restricted palette of at most 16 colours, defined once. Give flat layers distinct, muted colours: ground, roads by class group (major, minor, pedestrian), road_areas, rail, water, green, landuse.
  - Buildings take a colour by `building_type` group, through a small mapping from OSM `building` values to around 5 groups (residential, commercial/office, civic/religious, industrial, other/unknown) plus a landmark accent for buildings with a `landmark_id`.
  - Roofs and walls of the same building differ only through lighting, not through separate colours.
- **Per-building colour without extra draw calls:**
  - Use the `_feature_id` vertex attribute (named `_feature_id` in three) and a per-tile lookup: a `DataTexture` of colour indices built from the tile's feature table `<tile>.json`.
  - Inject the lookup with `onBeforeCompile` into one shared `MeshLambertMaterial` (`flatShading: true`) per layer, so the number of draw calls stays the same.
  - The feature table is fetched once per tile alongside the glb (extend the loader minimally if needed; T-3.2's loader already knows the tile id).
- **Lighting:** one fixed directional "sun" at a style-constant direction chosen so that the two visible wall faces at azimuth 45 degrees get clearly different shades (SimCity-like), plus a hemisphere or ambient fill. No time-of-day (that is Phase 4).
- **Cheap shadows:** one shadow map from the sun, fitted to the current view frustum's ground footprint and refitted on camera change (render on demand still). Use a bounded map size (style constant, at most 2048) and PCF, no cascades.
  - Make it switchable with `?shadows=0`, and measure the draw-call and frame-time impact with and without (headless numbers are indicative only; report them as such).
  - If shadows push the default view over 300 draw calls, report it.
- **Determinism for visual tests:** colours depend only on data and constants; no randomness.
- **Unit tests:**
  - the palette has at most 16 entries;
  - the building-type mapping covers every `building_type` in the fixture tables and maps unknown values to "other";
  - the colour-index texture builder gives the expected bytes for a small feature table.
- **e2e:** the existing specs pass, and a new screenshot of the fixture at a fixed camera is saved under `web/e2e-output/` (not yet a regression baseline; baselines come after the owner approves the look).
