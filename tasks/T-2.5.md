## Changes
1. **Content bounds (G2 issue 1).** Whole buildings overhang their grid cell by up to about 141 m. Add `content_bbox_local` {min: [x, y, z], max: [x, y, z]} per tile to `tiles.json`, computed from the dequantised vertex extents of every mesh in the tile. Update the schema. Add a data test that every decoded vertex lies inside its tile's `content_bbox_local` (tolerance 0.01 m).
2. **Flat-layer coverage (G2 issue 2).** Add a data test:
   - every source feature of the flat layers with area or length inside the processed extent appears in at least one tile (matched by layer and id);
   - pieces of one feature in different tiles do not overlap (area < 0.01 m2);
   - the summed tile area per area-layer equals the clipped source area within 0.1%.
3. **Simplification (G2 issue 3).** Replace the skip in `tests/data/test_data_simplification.py` with a real check: recompute the Hausdorff distance between every exported building volume footprint (decoded from the tiles) and its source volume, and assert <= 0.5 m. Report the maximum.
4. **Empty tiles (G2 issue 4).** A tile with no meshes must never be written as a 0-byte `.glb`. Either skip the tile entirely (and leave it out of the manifest) or write a valid empty glTF; choose skipping, and add a unit test.
5. **`make validate` (G2 issue 8)** depends on `data` as well as `tiles`. Since both are idempotent, this costs little when the cache is warm. Measure and report the warm-cache runtime.
