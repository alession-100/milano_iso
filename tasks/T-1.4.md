## Known problem to fix first (orchestrator evidence)
DBT volumes are currently selected one by one by intersection with the extent. A building on the edge of the extent therefore loses the volumes outside it, which breaks the "keep features whole" rule in ADR-0013. Measured on the current data: 44 of 706 OSM buildings have under 50% of their area covered by DBT volumes, and 24 have under 20%. Many are pre-2020 palazzi near the edge (for example `way/2788231` Palazzo Sormani, `way/7085622` Ca' de Sass).

**Fix:** select DBT buildings (`EDIFC` and `EDI_MIN`) that intersect the extent, then take **all** volumes of those buildings, wherever they lie. Apply this in `dbt_extract.py` and `normalise.py`. Rerun T-1.3's validation and show its numbers after the change. Volumes outside the extent are allowed only when their building intersects it; update the extent assertion accordingly.

## Rules
1. **DBT volumes** (ADR-0015). For each DBT building, `ground_z` is the minimum `base_z_min_m` of its `POR` 01 volumes. If it has none, use the minimum over all its volumes and set `ground_flag = "no_por01"`. Each volume then gets `bottom_m = (qe_m - av_m) - ground_z` and `top_m = qe_m - ground_z`, with `height_source = "dbt"`. Do not clamp: report any `bottom_m < -0.01` or `top_m <= bottom_m` in `outliers.csv`.
2. **Matching OSM to DBT.** For each OSM building, compute `coverage` = area(OSM footprint intersected with the union of all DBT volumes) / area(OSM footprint).
   - `coverage >= 0.5`: the OSM building is **matched** and is not rendered on its own.
   - Otherwise it is **unmatched** and rendered from its OSM footprint (rule 4).
   - The 0.5 threshold is provisional: report how many buildings fall between 0.3 and 0.7, so the threshold can be revisited.
3. **Attribute transfer.** Each DBT building takes `osm_type`, `osm_id`, `name` and `building_type` from the matched OSM building with the largest overlap area (ties broken by lower osm_id). It gets `landmark_id` when that OSM id is in `tests/landmarks.yaml`. Record the number of DBT buildings with no OSM attributes.
4. **Unmatched OSM buildings**, using the fallback chain in order; the first rule that applies wins:
   1. `osm_height`: parse `height_raw`. Accept a plain number, `N m`, `Nm`, `N ft` or `N'` (convert feet to metres); reject anything else. Log every value as accepted or rejected in `height_parse_log.csv`.
   2. `osm_levels`: `levels_raw` as a positive number times 3.0.
   3. Overture is skipped (ADR-0015).
   4. `default`: from `defaults.json` (rule 5).

   `bottom_m` comes from `min_height_raw`, or `min_level_raw` times 3.0, when present, otherwise 0. `building:part` objects are not rendered in v1; report how many unmatched buildings have parts.
5. **Defaults** (ADR-0015). Take the median of `buildings.height_m` over DBT buildings whose transferred `building_type` equals the value, when there are at least 5; otherwise the overall median of matched DBT buildings. Write `defaults.json` with value, n and method for every `building_type` that needs a default.

## Output layers
- `render_volumes`, one row per extrudable prism: geometry (2D polygon), `vol_key`, `building_key`, `source` (dbt/osm), `bottom_m`, `top_m`, `height_m` (= top - bottom), `height_source`, `ground_flag`.
- `buildings`, one row per building, geometry = union of its footprints: `building_key`, `source`, `height_m` (= max `top_m`), `height_source` (for DBT `dbt`; for OSM the rule used), `osm_type`, `osm_id`, `name`, `building_type`, `landmark_id`, `n_volumes`.
- Keys are stable strings (`dbt:<bld_id>`, `osm:<type><id>`). Rows sorted by key. Canonical-dump sha256 identical across two runs.

## Report (`heights.md` / `.json`)
- Counts and share by `height_source`, by building count and by footprint area.
- Distribution of `buildings.height_m`: min, p5, p25, median, p75, p95, max.
- Outliers (> 250 m or < 2 m) with keys and names.
- The tallest five buildings with keys, names and heights.
- Coverage histogram (0-0.1, ..., 0.9-1.0); the number of buildings between 0.3 and 0.7.
- `unmatched_osm.csv` with key, name, `building_type`, coverage, chosen rule and height.
