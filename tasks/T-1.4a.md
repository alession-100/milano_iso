# T-1.4a - Default height from minor buildings; do not render roofs or construction sites (corrective for T-1.4, retry 1/2)

- **Phase / depends on:** Phase 1 / T-1.4 (merged)
- **Owner:** executor
- **Branch:** t-1.4a

## Goal
Apply ADR-0016 to `pipeline/milano_iso/heights.py`.

## Evidence
`reports/artifacts/T-1.4/unmatched_osm.csv`: 18 of 22 unmatched OSM buildings got `default` heights of 28.41-34.15 m from `defaults.json` (the median of same-type DBT buildings). Among them are San Giovanni in Conca (`osm:w176512639`), Apple Piazza Liberty (`osm:w491367824`), the Galleria roof (`osm:w166623705`, `building=roof`) and a `building=construction` way (`osm:w1421353782`). The orchestrator measured the median of DBT `EDI_MIN` buildings in the pilot at 3.2 m (n = 47), against 29.75 m for `EDIFC` (n = 171).

## Inputs
- `CLAUDE.md`; `docs/DECISIONS.md` ADR-0015 and ADR-0016
- `pipeline/milano_iso/heights.py`, `tests/pipeline/test_heights.py`, `pipeline/mk/40-heights.mk`
- `reports/artifacts/T-1.4/*`
- Data: `processed/pilot.gpkg` (shared cache)

## Changes
1. **Default.** Compute the default as the median `height_m` of DBT buildings whose volumes have `building_class == "EDI_MIN"` in the processed extent. Record value, n and method in `defaults.json`. Remove the per-type default logic and its tests, replacing them with tests for the new rule, including the case of no `EDI_MIN` buildings: fail loudly with a clear error, never fall back silently.
2. **Not rendered.** Add `rendered` (bool) and `render_reason` (string or null) to `buildings` and `render_volumes`. Unmatched OSM buildings with `building_type` in {`roof`, `construction`} get `rendered = false` with reason `no_walls_roof` or `under_construction`. They keep a `height_m` and `height_source` (VERIFICATION requires both) but are excluded from rendering. Matched ones are unaffected.
3. Regenerate the T-1.4 artifacts. Add to `heights.md` a count of rendered and not-rendered buildings, with the reasons.
