## Specification
- **Camera model:**
  - Azimuth 45 degrees and elevation from `parseElevation` (default 30).
  - The camera always looks at a ground **target** point (x, 0, z) in client metres (ADR-0021 axes).
  - **Zoom** is the visible ground width in metres across the viewport.
  - The camera position is target + direction x distance. Keep the distance large and the near/far planes wide enough that buildings up to 350 m tall are never clipped. Choose and document the values.
  - Panning moves the target in the ground plane.
- **Defaults** are style constants in one exported object, not data: target (0, 0, 0) (the origin, the Duomo), initial visible width 600 m, zoom limits 60-4000 m. These are viewer settings, so label them "style/UX constants".
- **Bounds:** `setBounds({minX, maxX, minZ, maxZ})` clamps the target to the given rectangle, which T-3.2 provides from `tiles.json`. Before bounds are set, clamp to a +/-500 m square around the origin.
- **Mouse:**
  - Left drag pans, and the ground point under the cursor stays under the cursor (exact screen-to-ground mapping for an orthographic camera).
  - The wheel zooms about the cursor (the ground point under the cursor stays fixed), clamped to the zoom limits.
- **Touch:** one-finger drag pans; a two-finger pinch zooms about the midpoint; two-finger drag pans. Use Pointer Events.
- **Keyboard** (the canvas container is focusable, `tabindex=0`, with an aria-label from i18n): arrow keys pan by 10% of the visible width, `+`/`-` zoom by a factor of 1.25, and `0` resets the view.
- **Inertia:**
  - After a drag is released, the pan continues with exponential velocity decay. The time constant is a style constant of about 250 ms, and the motion stops below a threshold.
  - With `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` there is no inertia and zoom changes are instant.
  - Rendering is on demand: render only when something changes. No permanent requestAnimationFrame loop; `main.ts` exposes a `requestRender()`.
- **Test state** (`registerTestState('camera', ...)`): `{target: [x, z], visibleWidth, elevationDeg, azimuthDeg, bounds}`.
- **Unit tests (vitest):**
  - the screen-to-ground mapping round-trip at 30 and 35.264 degrees;
  - zoom-about-cursor keeps the ground point fixed (within 1e-6 m);
  - clamping to bounds;
  - zoom limits;
  - inertia decay reaches rest;
  - reduced motion disables inertia (inject the media query result).
- **e2e (`controls.spec.ts`, `?e2e=1`), on desktop 1280x720 and a Pixel 7-class mobile viewport with touch:**
  - a mouse drag of 200 px moves the target by the expected number of metres within 2%;
  - a wheel step zooms within the limits and keeps the ground point under the cursor fixed (within 1 m);
  - the arrow keys and `0` work;
  - dragging far beyond the bounds clamps;
  - on mobile, a touch pan via CDP `Input.dispatchTouchEvent` moves the target;
  - zero console errors (the ADR-0012 filter only).
