## Changes
1. **Merge at level 0.**
   - Level-0 tiles merge their flat layers into one mesh at load, as coarse tiles do.
   - Keep separate whatever must stay separate: buildings (the only shadow casters), instanced trees, and landmark details if needed. Say why for each.
   - Keep the `planDraw` count (`tileCalls`) in step with what is now drawn.

   - Measure the merge cost per level-0 tile (ms, median and max) and the time until the default view is complete, before and after.
2. **The plan.** Keep the T-6.5a shadow-frustum count if it holds up. The plan must never be below the real count. Report plan minus real (min, median, max).
3. **Dense sweep.** Run the T-6.5a dense sweep on the T-6.5 windows plus 1680x1050, 1920x1200 and 3440x1440:
   - widths every 40 m from 520 to 1,320 m, plus the existing widths;
   - ascending and descending order;
   - all five centres, rotation steps 0 and 1.

   Results go to `reports/artifacts/T-6.5b/sweep.json` and `sweep.md`, giving for each window:
   - max calls;
   - the views where the switch fired;
   - the worst pixel error on a switched view;
   - plan minus real.
4. **Default-view check and notch.**
   - `default-view-check.mjs`: level 0 and at most 300 calls on all nine windows.
   - At 1280x720, the notch 600 → 630 → 600 m must end on level 0.
5. **Transitional maxima.** Measure the most draw calls in any frame while zooming from 600 to 4,000 m at 10 Mbit/s, at 1280x720 and 1920x1080. These are recorded, not capped.
6. **Docs.**
