- **Phase / depends on:** Phase 6 follow-up, after T-6.6 and T-6.7 merged (main 9807013). Measurement only.
- **Owner:** executor
- **Branch:** t-6.8 (artifacts and report only)

## Goal
Two measurements owed before Phase 7, taken one after the other on a quiet machine:
1. The city bench, to settle whether the T-6.6 count costs frame rate. R-6.6 measured 1920x1080 at 85.4 and 77.8 fps while T-6.7 shared the GPU, against 101.3 fps in T-6.5b.
2. A one-hour memory session, to tell whether the renderer-process creep of R-6.7 levels off. At 20 minutes it was still about 2 to 3 MB/min.

## Background
- **R-6.6 bench:** the 1280x720 runs reached 118.7 and 89.8 fps (run 2 partly disturbed). Disturbed attempts are in `reports/artifacts/T-6.6/bench_contended*/` and `bench_disturbed/`.
- **R-6.7, 20-minute run:** renderer medians 183, 267 and 348 MB in minutes 1 to 3, then 357 to 408 MB over minutes 4 to 20. The JS heap, external memory, the GPU process and the object counts were flat or bounded.

## Changes
No code change. If a measurement script needs a fix to run for an hour, stop and report; do not change it.

1. **Bench.**
   - Build main and serve the city tileset.
   - Run `npm run bench:city` headed, on the real GPU: 1280x720 twice, then 1920x1080 twice.
