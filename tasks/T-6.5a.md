# T-6.5a - Budget switch: count level-0 draw calls accurately, so the detailed level is not dropped when it fits

- **Phase / depends on:** Phase 6 / T-6.5 merged (main 06cd422; ADR-0040). Corrective for G6 re-review blocker B2. **Attempt 2 of 2:** if this does not close B2, stop and escalate; do not try a third variant.
- **Owner:** executor

## Goal
The budget-aware level switch of ADR-0040 fires only when the level actually drawn would exceed the cap. Ordinary desktop views keep level 0, including the default view (origin, 600 m) on every common window. The settled-view budget of at most 300 still holds everywhere.

## Background (G6 re-review at 06cd422, reviewer's own probes)
- **The overestimate.** The level-0 plan counts the shadow pass over the view plus a ring of 250 m tiles. It overestimates the real level-0 calls by about 60-90, so the switch fires at about 190-200 real calls instead of 280.
- **The default view (origin, 600 m).**
  - At 1440x900, 1680x1050 and 1920x1200 it now draws level 1 (48 calls). Level 0 there draws only 204 real calls (pre-T-6.5 build).
  - Visible losses, side by side in `C:\Users\studi\AppData\Local\Temp\claude\d352876ecdc285bf\a1075ba5-a783-49d4-a1f1-5c7786945a17\scratchpad\shots\default_1440x900_before.png` and `default_1440x900_after.png`:
    - the Galleria glass roof;
    - the Duomo spires and other landmark details;
    - trees;
    - road markings.
  - 16:9 windows keep level 0 by only 2 plan calls (278 against 280).
- **Zoom notch at 1280x720.** 600 m draws level 0 (187 calls, plan 278). Going to 630 m switches to level 1, and back at 600 m it stays on level 1 (the hysteresis needs a plan of 252 or less).
- **1920x1080 origin, 640-940 m, and 2560x1440 origin, 640-1,260 m:** level 1 everywhere. Real level-0 calls before T-6.5 were 278 at 800 m and 302 at 900 m at 1920x1080, so the switch is genuinely needed near the top of that band, but not below it.
- **ADR-0040's measured claims are wrong,** because the T-6.5 sweep jumped from 600 m to 1,200 m:
  - worst error 2.00 px at 2560x1440, 640 m (2.13 px at 5120x1440), not 1.20 px;
  - it does not fire "rarely".
- **Reviewer evidence (read-only)** in the same scratchpad dir: `d_before.json`, `d_after.json`, `notch.json`, `b1.json`..`b3.json`, `transit/*.json`, `flicker.mjs` and the scripts in `scripts/` (including `probe.mjs`, which counts raw GL draws of one forced frame).

## Owner decision (2026-11-22): transitional frames
The 300 draw-call budget applies to **settled views** (every visible tile loaded, scene equal to loaded). Short peaks while a new level loads are accepted: the old tiles keep covering the ground (ADR-0037 cover rule) and reached 340-520 calls for a few seconds under 4-10 Mbit/s throttling. Record this:
- in `docs/VERIFICATION.md` section 5 (the draw-call row);
- in the new ADR;
- in the report, with a measured transitional maximum at 1280x720 and 1920x1080 under 10 Mbit/s.

No code change for transitional frames.

## Changes
1. **Accurate plan at every level, level 0 included.** Count what the renderer will actually issue:
   - for the shadow pass, the casters that the shadow camera will draw (the sun frustum used by `lights.ts`, or the casting meshes actually inside it), not a ring of tiles;
   - the main pass as now.

   The plan must never be below the real count. Aim for at most about 10 over it at every sweep view. Report the distribution of plan minus real.
2. **The switch fires only when the plan for the level that would otherwise be drawn exceeds the cap.**
   - Keep the cap at 280 and the hysteresis unless the measurements argue otherwise; give numbers.
   - At 1280x720 the zoom notch of 600 to 630 to 600 m must end on level 0.
3. **Denser sweep.**
   - Extend `web/scripts/sweep-city.mjs`: widths every 40 m from 520 to 1,320 m, plus the existing widths.
   - Run each window-and-centre group in ascending and in descending width order, so hysteresis paths are covered.
   - Add the windows 1440x900, 1680x1050 and 1920x1200 (16:10) and 3440x1440.
   - Results go to `reports/artifacts/T-6.5a/sweep.json` and `sweep.md`, giving for each window:
     - max calls;
     - the views where the switch fired;
     - the worst pixel error;
     - plan minus real (min, median, max).
4. **Default-view check.** A small test or script asserts that the default view (origin, 600 m, rotation step 0) draws level 0 and stays within 300 at these windows: 1280x720, 1366x768, 1440x900, 1536x864, 1600x900, 1680x1050, 1920x1080, 1920x1200 and 2560x1440. Put it in the sweep output, or in an e2e or script on the real tileset; it does not have to be in the default e2e.
5. **ADR.** Write a new ADR (next free number) that amends ADR-0040:
   - the corrected worst error and trigger count, measured;
   - the settled-view reading of the budget (owner decision above);
   - a note in ADR-0040's status line pointing to it.

   Correct `reports/artifacts/T-6.5/budgets.md`, or write `reports/artifacts/T-6.5a/budgets.md`.
