# T-5.2 - Rotation in 90-degree steps (four views)

- **Phase / depends on:** Phase 5 / G4 passed. Runs in parallel with T-5.3a.
- **Owner:** executor
- **Branch:** t-5.2

## Goal
Let the user turn the city in 90-degree steps, SimCity style. The four views have azimuths 45, 135, 225 and 315 degrees. The elevation does not change.

## Inputs
- `CLAUDE.md` (including Lessons learned); `docs/DECISIONS.md` ADR-0002, ADR-0003, ADR-0025, ADR-0026, ADR-0027, ADR-0030; `docs/VERIFICATION.md` sections 4, 4a and 5
- `web/src/camera.ts`, `web/src/controls.ts`, `web/src/tiles/culling.ts`, `web/src/style/lights.ts` (shadow fitting only), `web/src/main.ts`, `web/src/i18n.ts`, `web/src/testhook.ts`, and their tests
- `web/e2e/*`, `web/playwright.config.ts`

## Changes
1. **Camera.**
   - Make the azimuth part of the camera rig state: one of 4 steps, starting at 45 degrees (ADR-0003 is unchanged for the default view).
   - Rotate about the current view centre, so the target stays fixed.
   - Pan directions follow the screen: dragging right always moves the map right on screen, whatever the azimuth.
   - Bounds, zoom limits and inertia keep working in every view.
2. **Animation.**
   - A step animates over a style-constant duration of at most 400 ms, rendering on demand during the animation only.
   - With `prefers-reduced-motion` it is instant.
   - A step pressed during an animation is queued or snaps to the end state; the final azimuth is always exactly one of the 4 values.
3. **Input.**
   - Keys `Q` (counter-clockwise) and `E` (clockwise).
   - Two buttons with i18n labels and `aria-label`s (IT/EN): "Ruota a sinistra" / "Rotate left" and "Ruota a destra" / "Rotate right".
   - The buttons must be keyboard-reachable, at least 44x44 CSS px, and must not overlap the attribution, the tile notice or the day/night toggle at 412 px.
   - Put them in a delimited block in `main.ts` (`// --- T-5.2 rotation ---`), or in a small module. T-5.3b will later move all controls into one toolbar, so keep the button creation in one function.
4. **Rendering correctness.**
   - Tile culling, the shadow-map fit and the debug overlay readouts must be correct in all 4 views.
   - The sun stays fixed in the world, so shadows fall differently per view.
   - Show any culling bug with a test before fixing it.
5. **Test hook.**
   - Expose `azimuthDeg` in the existing camera test state.
   - Allow `__milanoIsoSetView` to take an optional azimuth.
   - Accept `?rot=0..3` as the initial step.
6. **Tests.**
   - Unit tests:
     - the step arithmetic (wrap-around both ways, always one of the 4 values);
     - rotating about the target keeps the target fixed;
     - pan direction per azimuth.
   - e2e:
     - Q and E and both buttons rotate;
     - 4 steps return to the start view;
     - tiles loaded and failed stay correct after rotation (no tile that should be visible is missing);
     - reduced motion gives an instant step;
     - zero console errors.
   - Visual: add the default view at `rot=1`, `rot=2` and `rot=3` at both sizes as new baselines.
     - The look is unchanged (ADR-0030), so these are derived views of the approved look.
     - Generate the win32 PNGs. Leave `*-linux.png` to the orchestrator.
