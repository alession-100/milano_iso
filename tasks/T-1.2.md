# T-1.2 - Fetch the Comune di Milano DBT 2020 and map its building/height schema

- **Phase / depends on:** Phase 1 / G0 passed. Runs in parallel with T-1.1 and T-1.5.
- **Owner:** executor
- **Branch:** t-1.2

## Goal
Download the DBT 2020 and record its checksum. Extract building footprints and volume units for the pilot AOI, and establish from the official specification exactly what the height attributes mean and how volumes relate to buildings.

## Inputs
- `CLAUDE.md`
- `docs/DECISIONS.md`: ADR-0004, ADR-0009, ADR-0013 only.
- `docs/DATA_SOURCES.md`: row 3a, row 3b and note 3 only.
- `pipeline/milano_iso/config.py`
- `pyproject.toml`, `tests/pipeline/` (conventions)

## Outputs
- `pipeline/milano_iso/dbt_fetch.py` and `pipeline/milano_iso/dbt_extract.py` (`python -m milano_iso.dbt_extract --extent pilot`)
- `pipeline/manifests/dbt2020.json`, `pipeline/manifests/dbt_spec.json`
- `pipeline/mk/20-dbt.mk`: target `data-dbt`, appended to `DATA_STEPS`.
- `tests/pipeline/test_dbt_extract.py` (synthetic data only)
- `reports/artifacts/T-1.2/`:
  - `schema.md`: every layer in the ZIP with its geometry type, CRS and feature count. For `EDIFC` and `UN_VOL`, every field with its type, null count and min/median/max for numerics.
  - `height_semantics.md`
  - `summary.json`
- `reports/R-1.2.md`

## Specification
- **Download.** Fetch `https://gisportal.comune.milano.it/download/area_download/SIT/DBT2020/DBT_2020.zip` (the EPSG:7791 version) to `data/raw/dbt2020/`, and the regional specification PDF named in DATA_SOURCES row 3a to `data/raw/dbt_spec/`. Write a manifest for each (url, retrieved_utc, bytes, sha256). Downloads must be idempotent: skip when the sha256 matches. Use a generic User-Agent `milano-iso-pipeline/0.1`.
  - **Shared helper with T-1.1.** If `pipeline/milano_iso/fetch.py` does not exist on your branch, write your own minimal helper inside `dbt_fetch.py`; T-1.1 writes `fetch.py` in parallel and the orchestrator will unify the two later. Do not create `fetch.py` yourself.
- **Read without unzipping the whole archive** where possible (GDAL `/vsizip/`).
  - Extract `EDIFC` and `UN_VOL` features that intersect the pilot extent: `PILOT_AOI_BBOX`, transformed from EPSG:32632 into the layer's CRS with densified edges.
  - Keep them whole and in the source CRS, in `data/interim/dbt_pilot.gpkg` with layers `edifc` and `un_vol`.
  - Rows sorted by a stable source key (state which key).
- **Height semantics** (`height_semantics.md`). Answer each point by citing the specification PDF with page numbers and quoting under 15 words each:
  1. What `UN_VOL_AV` measures: height above ground, eave, ridge, or relative to `UN_VOL_QE`.
  2. What `UN_VOL_QE` measures.
  3. Units.
  4. How `UN_VOL` relates to `EDIFC`: which key, and whether the relation is one-to-many.
  5. Meaning of `UN_VOL_POR` and any other code fields needed to tell above-ground volumes from underground or portico ones.
  6. The meaning of the 287 zero-height records and the negative minimum seen in the audit.

  Then check each point empirically on the pilot extract: the join match rate, the number of volumes per building, and the counts per code value. If the spec does not answer a point, write `NOT IN SPEC` and give the empirical observation separately, labelled as an observation.
- **CRS.** Report the transformation pyproj selects from EPSG:7791 to EPSG:32632 (`TransformerGroup` operations, with the accuracy for each) and state its expected positional effect. Do not reproject the outputs; T-1.3 does that.

## Acceptance criteria
1. `make data-dbt` succeeds. A second run skips the downloads and produces the same canonical-dump sha256 for `dbt_pilot.gpkg`; show both hashes.
2. Both manifests are complete.
3. `schema.md` covers every layer in the ZIP and full field statistics for `EDIFC` and `UN_VOL`.
4. `height_semantics.md` answers points 1-6, each with a page citation, or `NOT IN SPEC` plus an observation.
5. `summary.json` gives the pilot counts for `edifc` and `un_vol` and the join match rate.
6. `make test` exits 0.
7. `git status --short` is empty; work committed on `t-1.2`.

## Escalate if
- The ZIP or PDF URL fails or has changed size from DATA_SOURCES (596,533,009 bytes and 12,386,999 bytes).
- `EDIFC` or `UN_VOL` is not in the ZIP, or the join between them cannot be established.
- The spec says the heights are not above-ground heights in metres.
