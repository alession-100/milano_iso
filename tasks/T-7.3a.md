# T-7.3a - Probe deploy to Cloudflare Pages: are the tiles compressed, and does the phone view fit 3 MB?

- **Phase / depends on:** Phase 7. The owner approved the probe deploy on 2026-11-24 (ADR-0045, accepted on condition).
- **Owner:** executor. The owner does the account steps first (below).
- **Branch:** t-7.3a (scripts, report and artifacts only)

## Owner steps (before the executor starts)
1. Create a free Cloudflare account.
2. Choose the Pages project name. It becomes `https://<name>.pages.dev`.
3. On this machine, run `npx wrangler@4.159.0 login` and complete the browser sign-in yourself. The executor never handles credentials.

## Goal
Measure what the real host serves, so ADR-0045's condition is decided by numbers:
- whether `.glb` tiles arrive with a `Content-Encoding`, and which;
- the phone default view's bytes on the wire against 3,000,000 B.

## Changes
1. **Build.** Build main as of this card, with the city tileset, into a fresh directory outside the repo.
   - Leave out `.br` and `.gz` copies, to stay under the 20,000-file limit (R-7.0).
   - Count the files and confirm the count is under 20,000.
2. **Deploy.** Deploy the build with `wrangler pages deploy <dir> --project-name <owner's name>`, using the owner's login.
   - Use one deploy (a production deploy is acceptable; this is the owner-approved probe).
   - Record the deployment URL and its id.
3. **Headers.** Request with `curl -sI` and `Accept-Encoding: br, gzip, zstd`, and record `Content-Type`, `Content-Encoding`, `Cache-Control`, `Vary` and size, for:
   - `index.html` and one JS asset;
   - the root `tiles.json`;
   - one shard and `index.json`;
   - three `.glb` tiles at levels 0, 1 and 3;
   - one feature table;
   - `minimap.svg`.

   Repeat with `Accept-Encoding: gzip` only, as an older client sends.

   Check that the `_headers` rules took effect: `immutable` on `/assets/*` and `/tiles/*`, and `no-cache` on `index.html` and `tiles.json`.
4. **Payload.** Run `web/scripts/measure-payload.mjs`, or its existing method, against the deployed URL. Do this at 412x915 and 1280x720, for the default view, three runs each, and record the bytes on the wire until the view is complete.
5. **If `.glb` tiles arrive uncompressed:** do not try workarounds. Report the figures and stop. The options (Netlify, a paid compression rule, decompressing in the client) are the owner's decision.

## Constraints

- **Scope of the deploy:** no deploy other than the one probe deploy (a second only if the first fails mid-upload; say so). Do not change any Cloudflare setting, and do not add a custom domain or anything paid.
- **No changes** to app code, the pipeline or dependencies.
- **Processes:** never stop a process you did not start. The owner's servers are on 4173 and 4174.
- **Commit messages** end with `Co-Authored-By: Claude Sonnet 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`.

- `.glb` tiles are not compressed.
- The phone view is over 3,000,000 B.
- The upload fails twice.
- Wrangler asks for anything beyond the owner's existing login.
