## Specification
- **Off by default:** zero cost when the flag is absent. Do not load or construct the overlay without `?debug`; the import may be dynamic.
- **Measuring mode.** With `?debug`, the app normally renders on demand, which makes fps meaningless. The overlay therefore offers a **benchmark mode**:
  - the button "Benchmark" (i18n) or `?debug=bench` runs a fixed scripted camera path for 10 s with continuous rendering: a pan across the pilot area plus a zoom from 600 m to 4000 m and back;
  - it then reports the mean fps, the p5 fps (the 5th percentile of frame rate), the mean and p95 frame time, and the max draw calls and triangles;
  - the results are shown in the overlay and exposed through `registerTestState('bench', ...)`;
  - the path is deterministic (fixed keyframes).
- **Live readouts** (updated after each rendered frame): the draw calls, the triangles, the tile stats, the camera target and visible width, and the device pixel ratio and canvas size.
- **Layout:** the overlay must not cover the attribution; it is readable on a 412-px-wide phone and has a close button.
- **Keyboard:** the overlay is reachable with Tab and the benchmark button works with Enter.
- **Unit tests:**
  - the statistics (mean, p5, p95) are correct on known arrays;
  - the benchmark path keyframes are deterministic.
- **e2e (`debug.spec.ts`, fixture tiles):**
  - with `?debug&e2e=1` the overlay is visible and shows numeric draw calls;
  - with `?debug=bench&e2e=1` the bench state completes within 20 s and holds numeric results;
  - without `debug` there is no overlay element in the DOM;
  - zero console errors (shared filter in `e2e/support.ts`).
