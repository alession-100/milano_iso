# T-6.5 - Draw calls within 300 on every settled city view: fewer meshes per coarse tile, plus a budget-aware level switch

- **Phase / depends on:** Phase 6 / T-6.4 merged (main 3fa0a44). Corrective for gate G6 blocker B1 (first G6 review, 2026-11-20). Attempt 1 of 2.
- **Owner:** executor
- **Branch:** t-6.5

## Goal
Every settled view of the city stays within 300 draw calls, on any window size from 360x640 to 2560x1440, at any centre inside the comune, any rotation step and any zoom from 60 to 4,000 m. The look must not change.

## Background (G6 B1, numbers from the reviewer's settled-view probes)
- Each tile draws one mesh per layer, plus a shadow pass for the building mesh. At level 2 that comes to about 4.3 calls per tile with shadows off and about 5.4 with them on.
- Level 2 covers 1,920-3,839 m at 1280 px, and no settled view in that range was ever measured.
- Settled views at 1280x720, all drawing level 2 only:

- The reviewer's probe results are in `C:\Users\studi\AppData\Local\Temp\g6rev\probe1.json` to `probe5.json`, and the benches in `bench1280.json` and `bench1920.json`. These are read-only references; use them to check that your sweep reproduces their numbers before you change anything.

## Owner decision (2026-11-20): both fixes
1. **Fewer meshes per coarse tile** (levels 1 to 3). Merge each coarse tile's layers into as few meshes as possible; one or two is the aim. The rendered colours must be the same as now:
   - per-building lookup colours;
   - flat layer colours;
   - water;
   - markings, with their depth and offset handling;
   - day and night mode switching (`materials.ts` recolours shared flat materials in place).

   Picking must still return the same feature keys at every level, and shadow casting stays buildings only. Level 0 (the base tiles) is not changed: its glb and json stay byte-identical. Whether the merge happens in the pipeline (coarse glb layout) or in the client at load time is your choice. Write down the trade-off you measured: draw calls, load time, memory, and determinism if you choose the pipeline.
2. **Budget-aware level switch** as a safety net. If the planned draw for a view would exceed a cap, the client draws the next coarser level instead, which bends the 1-pixel error rule of ADR-0037.
   - The plan is the `planDraw` result, counted as the calls it will issue, so the count is deterministic and known before drawing.
   - Use a cap of 280 calls unless your measurements argue for another value. Give the number and the reason.
   - Add hysteresis so the level does not flicker.
   - Never go coarser than level 3.
   - Record the worst error in pixels that this allows, measured. Write this up as an amendment of ADR-0037 in a new ADR.
   - The switch must rarely be needed. Report how many sweep views it triggers on before and after fix 1.
