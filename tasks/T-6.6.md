# T-6.6 - Exact draw-call count for loaded tiles, so the budget switch fires only where it is needed

- **Phase / depends on:** Phase 6 follow-up, after G6 PASS WITH RISKS (main 3097949). It closes accepted risks R1 and R2 of the third G6 review (ADR-0041 status line).
- **Owner:** executor
- **Branch:** t-6.6

## Goal
The budget switch (ADR-0040, ADR-0041) decides from the calls a view will really issue. Once a view's tiles are loaded, the count is exact. The switch then drops a level only where the level it would otherwise draw really exceeds the cap, and never further than needed.

## Background
- The plan comes from the manifest. It counts every merged mesh of every tile whose content sphere meets the camera frustum, plus a shadow count, plus `NON_TILE_CALLS` = 3. three.js culls each mesh by its own bounding sphere, so the plan is a safe upper bound but loose: plan minus real is 1 / 21 / 133 (min / median / max) in `reports/artifacts/T-6.5b/sweep.json`.
- **R1, 1920x1200:** level 0 is dropped between about 880 and 940 m. Real level-0 cost is 224-235 calls, the plan is 270-297, and the cap is 280. That is 32 of 192 views over 6 centres and rotations 0-3, with errors of 1.02-1.09 px.
- **R2, 3840x2160 at 4,000 m:** the switch jumps from base level 1 to level 3, an error of 2.88 px. Level 1 really costs 316-353 calls; whether level 2 fits was not measured. At 1,200 m, level 0 costs 296-303.
- **On 2,560 px and wider windows,** 346 of 375 switched views did not need the switch (R-6.5b `switch_need.json`).
- **Design sketch from R-6.5a,** your call:
  - Count loaded tiles from their actual mesh bounding spheres against the camera frustum and the sun shadow frustum. Tiles not yet loaded keep the conservative manifest count.
  - Treat "over the cap only because of unloaded tiles" carefully, so the switch neither fails to load a level that would fit nor ping-pongs as tiles arrive.
  - Your design must say what it does with:
    - level-0 tiles loaded and then dropped in a genuinely heavy band;
    - what you see depending on the cache;
    - views that never settle.

## Changes
1. **Count.** Write an exact count for loaded tiles, with the treatment of unloaded tiles stated. It must never be below the real count once a view is settled. Keep `NON_TILE_CALLS` only if still needed.
2. **Stable switch.** The switch uses the new count with the existing cap (280) and hysteresis, unless you measure a reason to change them. It must not ping-pong:
   - on a settled view, the drawn level does not change while the camera is still;
   - during a pan, at most one change per crossing of a threshold.
3. **Smallest step.** When a switch is needed, it goes to the finest coarser level that fits, not straight to the coarsest. Report how this does at 3840x2160.
4. **Sweep.** Extend `web/scripts/sweep-city.mjs`, or add a probe on the same lines, to cover what the third review found missing:
   - 1920x1200 at 6 centres, rotation steps 0 to 3, widths every 20 m from 840 to 1,000 m;
   - 3840x2160 at 1,200, 3,000, 3,600 and 4,000 m at 3 centres.

   Keep the T-6.5b dense sweep as well (11 windows, 520-1,320 m every 40 m, both orders). Results go to `reports/artifacts/T-6.6/`.
5. **Docs.** Write an ADR (next free number) amending ADR-0040 and ADR-0041, with:
   - the new count;
   - the measured worst error;
   - the switch firings, with the number of views where it was needed versus fired.

   Correct the figures in the ADR-0041 status line by pointing to the new ADR.
