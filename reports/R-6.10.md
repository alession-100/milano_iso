## Deviations and assumptions
- The card names `memory-analysis.mjs` for the Playwright rule; ADR-0044 (b) says `memory-session.mjs`. The rule lives in `memory-analysis.mjs` (used by `memory-session.mjs`); the ADR status line says so. `memory-session.mjs` was not edited, so its header comment still mentions a renderer percent limit and 6 MB/min (stale text).
- Ceiling taken as 250/60 = 4.1667 MB/min (the ADR writes 4.2 as the rounded figure); fitted after the 180 s warm-up as before.
- Frame reference: frames per second of the reference run's last sample (122,785 at 1197 s) times this run's last sample time. The 25 % band is on the ratio.
- Hidden check: a sample without a `visibility` field counts as not visible. A slope fitted on fewer than 3 samples after minute 20 fails the run (no measurement). A run of exactly 20 minutes has no such samples, so in practice a pass needs a run clearly longer than 20 minutes (ADR: 60 for a gate). The ADR is silent on this.
- `docs/VERIFICATION.md` does not describe the memory check beyond the G6 line, so it was not changed.
