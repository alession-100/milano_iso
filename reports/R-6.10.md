## Deviations and assumptions
- The card names `memory-analysis.mjs` for the Playwright rule, ADR-0044 (b) says `memory-session.mjs`; the rule lives in `memory-analysis.mjs`, which `memory-session.mjs` uses (stated in the ADR status line).
- Frame reference: frames per second of the reference run's last sample times this run's last sample time; the 25 % band is on the ratio.
- A sample without a `visibility` field counts as not visible.
- `docs/VERIFICATION.md` does not describe the memory check beyond the G6 line, so it is unchanged.
- The 4.17 MB/min first version of the ceiling (first pass, 05effd7) is superseded by the owner's 6 / 12 MB/min.
