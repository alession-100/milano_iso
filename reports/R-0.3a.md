- Working-tree files use CRLF on this machine (git warns about CRLF to LF normalisation); the repository content is LF.
- "Wikidata distance" changed from 83.3 to 83.4 m as a consequence; the note in DATA_SOURCES still refers to the T-0.3 50 m threshold (still exceeded).
