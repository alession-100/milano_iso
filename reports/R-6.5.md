## Deviations and assumptions
- **The merge is in the client, not the pipeline** (the card left the choice): trade-off measured and written in ADR-0040 (0.25 to 0.98 ms per tile, no tile file or hash changes).
- **Only the flat layers are merged; buildings stay a separate mesh** (shadow casting is buildings only). A coarse tile is 2 meshes plus the shadow pass, not 1; the card said "one or two".
- **The plan counts every layer as shown and shadows as on** (below level 3, and not with `?shadows=0`); layer toggles only lower the real count. The shadow part is counted over the view plus its prefetch ring, because the sun's shadow frustum reaches past the view (measured: plan >= calls everywhere but by at most 2). This makes the plan conservative at level 0 and caused 2 unneeded switches (1440x900, 600 m, origin). I kept the conservative rule: it is what keeps every settled view under 300.
- **Cap 280** kept (card default): the plan was never more than 2 calls under the real count.
- **`ReadPixels` console note** filtered in the sweep as ADR-0012 does for e2e.
- **Rerun groups** replaced in the sweep JSON files (see above); the 'before' file has no reruns.
- **Window 2560x1440 only**: the sweep ends at 2560 CSS px; wider windows are not measured.
- **Sweep order:** widths ascending on one page per (window, centre, step), so the level hysteresis is the same on every run; a descending zoom (finer after coarser) with the budget hysteresis is covered by the vitest only, not by a sweep view.
- A "fix 1 alone" build is main plus this branch's merge with `cap: 1e9` set locally (not committed), built to `dist-fix1`.
- The wall time of e2e run 1 is not a measure of the suite (see above).
- `docs/PROGRESS.md`: only the T-6.5 row is set to "review"; the gate status text is the orchestrator's.

## Open questions and risks
- **Level-0 false positives:** the conservative shadow ring switches 2 views (1440x900, 600 m) to level 1 at 1.20 px although 204 calls would have fit. If the owner prefers fewer such switches, the ring can be replaced by the sun frustum itself, with the risk of an undercount. Decision for the orchestrator.
- **Frames while loading** are outside the cap (a coarser tile stays under the finer ones): the benches (max 241) show no breach, but there is no sweep of transitional frames.
- **Merged mesh depth ties:** two pixels per view change colour (above); a view dominated by coplanar layers could show more. None of the 8 views does.
- The cap makes the level depend on the window size (2560 px wide windows get level 2 up to 3,830 m instead of level 1 beyond 3,000 m): an accepted bend of the 1-pixel rule, at most 1.20 px.
- The sweep takes about 1 hour on this machine (880 views, software GL); it is not part of CI.
