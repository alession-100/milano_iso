## Deviations and assumptions
- `.gitignore` edited (`!tests/data/`): without it `tests/data/` is ignored by the `data/` rule and could not be committed. Not in the card's output list, but needed.
- `pyproject.toml` edited for the marker and `addopts`; the card says to configure the marker but does not name the file.
- `check-determinism` calls the step functions directly (`osm_extract.extract`, `dbt_extract.extract_pilot`, `normalise.run`, `heights.run`) into scratch directories under `$MILANO_ISO_DATA/_determinism` (deleted afterwards), mirroring the `.mk` recipes, rather than invoking the Make targets. Reason: the Make recipes write to the real `interim/`, `processed/` and committed `reports/artifacts/T-1.x` paths, which this task must not modify. The script requires the step list from `$(DATA_STEPS)` and exits 2 for an unknown step, so a new step cannot be silently skipped. `data-boundary` is a download-only step and produces no GeoPackage. The osm and dbt extracts are re-run from the raw downloads (not skipped), since "every step" includes them; the raw downloads are only read.
- Hashes use `milano_iso.geo.canonical_dump_sha256` for all four GeoPackages (the osm extract's own `canonical_sha256` is a different function).
- OSM completeness: "matched" is computed independently as coverage of the processed OSM footprint by the union of dbt `render_volumes` >= 0.5. The 0.5 value is imported from `heights.MATCH_THRESHOLD` (a constant only). OSM denominator: `building` tag present and not `no`.
- Extent check: for `dbt_volumes` the "building" is the `dbt_buildings` footprint; for `render_volumes` it is the `buildings` row. All features passed.
- Landmark (b) for DBT buildings uses the union of the building's rendered volumes. Centroids are computed after subtracting the map origin to avoid precision loss at about 5e6 m.
- The galleria entry is reported as `deferred_phase4` only when the building row has `rendered = false` (it does, `no_walls_roof`); it is a pytest skip, not a pass.
- `junit.xml` and the other artifacts carry run-specific timestamps, so they differ on every run.
- The `landmarks_check.csv` / summary paths can be overridden by env var; the committed ones come from the real-data run.

Either relax or redefine the (b) criterion by ADR, or fix the matching; I did not change the method.
