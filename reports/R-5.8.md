## Deviations and assumptions
- N9 design: the overlay moved to the bottom (above the band) at all widths, not only below 700 px (the old right column at wide widths pushed the card over the zone at 700-940 px). In compact sizes it shows only its title bar; live numbers are hidden there (use a taller or wider window to read them). Assumption: acceptable for a dev tool; ADR-0034 only says "above the band".
- The extra "re-scroll the active option when the list height changed" in `fit()` was needed: under load, a keypress before the first fit scrolled for the natural 220 px list and the later squeeze left the option out of view.
- N8: the review's exact figures (31 of 44 px etc.) were not reproduced one by one; the test asserts the whole option is visible for all 8 options in all three cases. The old code failed these only under load (see criterion 3).
- Python edits wrote CRLF into `layout.ts`/`debug.ts` working copies; git normalises to LF (`.gitattributes`), diffs are minimal.
- The note "160 tests" counts everything the suite reports; the earlier suite size was not re-measured by me.
