### Unit tests (new, `budget.test.ts`)
- "counts a loaded tile from the meshes that meet the view, not from its manifest"
- "takes a level whose bound is over the cap but whose loaded tiles fit, and does not go coarser"
- "with some tiles loaded and some not, the count is never below the frame the loaded ones give once all are" (loaded and unloaded mix, never under real)
- "does not change level while the camera is still, whatever arrives"
- "a pan across the cap changes the level exactly where the count crosses the cap or its hysteresis, not more" (no ping-pong)

- "a tile of the level in use that is outside the view and its ring is not drawn and not counted, whatever the cache holds"
- "asks again when tiles arrive after a decision that waited for them, and not otherwise"

## Deviations and assumptions
1. **First r2 probe exited 1** (`done.txt` "r2 exit 1"; first output kept outside the repo in `t66data/r2_first.*`): the probe's own settle check failed, not the app. `sweep-city.mjs` exits 1 when a view is not settled. 1 of 48 views, the first of the page (3840x2160, (0,0), step 0, 1,200 m, asc), was still waiting when the cold start timed out (61 level-0 and 3 level-1 meshes drawn, estimate 80 against 227 calls, `settled: false`). Everything else in that run was exact. I reran the whole probe on an idle server: 48 views, 0 not settled, exit 0; the rerun is `parts/r2.*`.
2. **Dense sweep in parts:** the 11 windows were run as 11 invocations by the previous executor (`parts/*.json`) and combined by `sweep-t66-report.mjs`. 1920x1200 appears with 1,032 views (dense 600 + R1 432). `switch-need` was run on the three windows where the switch fired (2560x1440, 3440x1440, 3840x2160); the others have no switched view and no view over 280.
3. **Bench was disturbed by the machine.** T-6.7 runs a headed memory session on the same GPU, and other load exists. Several attempts collapsed in later segments with unchanged draw calls (1920x1080 54.9 fps with p1 low 4.5; 1280x720 21.5 fps); they are kept in `bench_contended2/`, `bench_contended3/` and `bench_disturbed/` and are not the evidence. The 1280x720 run 2 (89.8 fps mean, one segment at 18.6 fps) is also partly disturbed but meets mean at least 60. The 1920x1080 mean (78-85 fps) is below the 101 fps of T-6.5b at the same size; I attribute it to load and the shared GPU but did not isolate it, and a bench of the pre-T-6.6 build under the same load was not run.
4. **No-ping-pong on a slow pan:** covered by the vitest pan test and by the sweep orders asc and desc on one page; not measured with a real slow drag in a browser.
5. `NON_TILE_CALLS` was lowered from 3 to 1: the count of loaded tiles is the frame's own, and 7,080 views have count minus real 0.
6. The ADR says "to be confirmed by review"; I do not sign off.
