## Design choices
- **Placement is rule-based, not by DBT id.** A spec selects volumes by rules over the landmark's own rendered volumes (tallest, `top_m` fraction of the tallest, footprint area), so the same spec runs on synthetic volumes and on real data. Details rest on the highest rendered volume of the landmark that covers the anchor point; `base_m` is always a `top_m` from the data.
- **Duomo:** 1 main spire on the volume with the greatest `top_m` (`dbt:UN_VOL_67987`, top 94.579 m), 18 turret spires on the slender tall volumes (area <= 25 m2, top >= 35% of the tallest), 91 pinnacles along the outer edge of the rendered massing inset 0.6 m at 7 m spacing. 110 details.
- **Torre Velasca:** 11 pitched crowns (hip roof, rise 3.5 m) on the exposed top of each volume with top >= 85% of the tallest (100.179 m) and area >= 10 m2. "Exposed" = the volume footprint minus every volume with a higher `(top_m, key)`, so crowns never overlap.
- **Roofs from a heightfield:** hip roofs and the vault share one routine: Delaunay triangulation of the densified boundary, a regular interior grid and the pole of inaccessibility, kept where the triangle centroid is inside the polygon, height = f(distance to the edge). Shared vertices, so the surface is watertight and orientation is upward.
- **Client colour uses the existing per-tile lookup material** (one material per tile mesh, one shared program), so the extra draw calls are one per tile with details. Details cast no shadows (style choice, keeps the shadow pass unchanged); they receive them.

## Galleria glass roof (change 2): rule and numbers
- **Footprint:** OSM way 166623705 (`building=roof`, "Galleria Vittorio Emanuele Secondo"), taken from `osm_buildings`; exactly one row (the export fails otherwise). Area 4,089.9 m2, perimeter 529.7 m.
- **Adjacent volumes (rule):** rendered DBT volumes with at least 10 m2 of their footprint inside the roof footprint buffered by 1 m (both numbers are style constants). Result: 8 volumes, all of one DBT building (`dbt:EDIFC_4893`, so the identification is unambiguous; the export fails if they ever belong to more than one building): UN_VOL_58493 26.708, 58501 25.750, 58504 36.708, 58507 32.742, 58519 28.847, 70912 29.431, 71592 30.833, 98657 30.770 (`top_m`, m).
- **Base (eave) = the lowest `top_m` of those volumes = 25.750 m** (UN_VOL_58501). Rationale: the glass springs from the lowest cornice and the higher neighbouring facades rise above it.
- **Vault:** z = eave + rise * sqrt(1 - (1 - min(d/W, 1))^2), d = distance to the footprint edge, W = A/P = 7.72 m (the half-span, taken from the footprint), rise = 0.6 * W = 4.63 m (0.6 is a style constant). One detail, 1,668 triangles in tile `-1_0` together with other details.
- The data test `test_galleria_vault_base_is_the_lowest_adjacent_eave` recomputes the rule independently of the exporter and compares it with the decoded mesh (lowest vertex 25.75 m +/- 0.03 m).

## Deviations and assumptions
1. **Schema:** only a `description` annotation on `layers` (see above), because `layers` is an unconstrained name list. I did not add a `contains` rule: unit tests build manifests with other layer lists.
2. **The glb as a whole changes** (new mesh). "Byte-identical buildings" is proven per layer (mesh and feature table hashes), not by the glb file hash, which cannot stay equal when a mesh is added. Tiles without details keep their glb bytes (the other 25 of 30).
3. **Placement test definition:** "within the landmark's rendered footprint union buffered <= 2 m" is applied to all detail vertices (incl. the dequantisation error). For the Galleria, which has no rendered volume with its id (ADR-0018, deferred), the region is its OSM roof footprint. The "base not below the volume it sits on" test uses the anchor point (centre of the lowest ring) for prisms and each lowest vertex for roofs; a point counts as on a volume when 0.05 m inside its footprint.
4. **Known visual limitations:** a pinnacle or spire whose ring crosses a neighbouring small volume is partly buried in it (the anchor, not every ring vertex, rests on the highest volume). The vault outline is slightly ragged along concave corners (Delaunay, centroid filter). The vault overlaps about 3% of its area with DBT volumes (134 m2, hidden inside them).
5. **Style picks not fixed by the card:** glass kinds get a separate tint (`landmarkGlass`); details cast no shadows; spec rules and all numbers in the table above; the choice of "turret" and "crown" selectors; San Siro tower count 8 (not the real count); masts/crowns/slabs for the CityLife, Pirelli towers are generic placeholders.
6. **Linux baselines** are stale for the changed fixture (tile `-1_0`); CI's visual check will fail until the orchestrator regenerates them (as after T-4.1).
7. Line endings: files I patched with scripts have CRLF in the Windows working copy; git normalises them on commit (warnings seen). No functional effect.
8. PROGRESS.md and DECISIONS.md not edited (orchestrator owns them); no ADR is proposed by this task.
