## Deviations and assumptions
1. **Owner-accepted:** 4 cm unit on levels 2 and 3 (worst offsets in the table), coarse heights rounded to a step of twice the tolerance (a volume that would round to nothing gets half a step; a volume wholly below ground is not drawn). Both are in ADR-0037 (points 3, 5).
2. **Level 3 tolerance 3 m instead of 5 m** (my decision, needed for the draw-call budget after the split; it also makes level 3 finer and bigger: 10.0 MB instead of 6.6 MB). Thresholds 0.5 / 1.5 / 3 m.
3. The split uses the pipeline's Python zlib figure; another zlib could split a tile near the limit differently, which would matter for Linux byte identity (CI).
4. The split stops after 4 levels and fails the build; the fixture test uses a budget of 860 B to force splits.
5. Existing e2e assertions on the whole `tiles` state (`rotation.spec.ts`, one in `picking.spec.ts`) use `toMatchObject` since the state gained fields.
6. Node's zlib gives about 20% more than Python's for the same .glb; the budget figure is the pipeline's. Measured on a software renderer; `meazza` and `east4500` are my points (inside the comune).
7. Pilot gets its coarse levels as the card says; its minimap is unchanged.

## Open questions and risks
- Linux byte identity of the coarse tiles (incl. split decisions) is unproven (CI).
- Level 3 headroom is 92 m in the 4 cm range.
- Real GPU/phone frame rate at levels 2 and 3 (about 1.6 M triangles at 1280x720 2400 m on level 2, 1.5 M at 4000 m on level 3) is T-6.4.
