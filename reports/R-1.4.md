## Deviations and assumptions
- **OSM type letters.** The pipeline stores `osm_type` as `w`/`r`/`n`, so keys read `osm:w2788231`, not `osm:way2788231`. `tests/landmarks.yaml` spells types out; `load_landmarks` maps them to the letters.
- **`landmark_id` for OSM-only buildings** is also set when their OSM id is a landmark (the card only specifies it for DBT buildings).
- **Edge selection.** A building "intersects the extent" if its `EDIFC`/`EDI_MIN` footprint (IS or ME layer) or any of its volumes does. Volumes are read from the full `UN_VOL` shapefile and filtered by `CEDIUV`.
- **Parsing.** Units are case-sensitive (`m`, `ft`, `'`); a zero height is rejected for `height`; commas, ranges, feet-inches and `;` lists are rejected. The parse log covers the values of unmatched OSM buildings only (those that reach rule 1, 2 or the bottom rule); matched buildings are not rendered from OSM and their tags are not parsed. In the pilot only 4 values exist (1 height, 3 levels), none rejected; rejection paths are covered by unit tests.
- **Min-height guard.** If an OSM `min_height` were not below the top, `height_m` would be <= 0 and `heights` would exit 1 via `check_layers`. No such case occurs in the pilot; I did not invent a handling rule.
- `buildings.osm_id` is a nullable integer; GeoPackage read-back gives float64 for it when nulls exist (values exact).
- Overlap ties in attribute transfer are compared after rounding areas to 1e-6 m2.
- Underground DBT volumes (`POR` 08) are not excluded (none in the pilot).
- Percentiles use numpy linear interpolation.
- Rerunning `dbt_extract` rewrites `reports/artifacts/T-1.2/summary.json` (counts now reflect whole-building volume selection); its line endings are also converted.

## Open questions and risks
- **Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II** (`osm:w166623705`, a landmark in the pilot, `building=roof`) has coverage 0.033, so it is unmatched and gets the `default` height of 28.41 m (the overall median, because the `roof` type has n=1). It is a roof over an arcade, so it is probably wrong; it is the only listed pilot landmark rendered from a default. A decision on landmark-specific handling is needed in a later task.
- **Defaults.** 18 of 22 unmatched buildings (7.5% of buildings, 0.94% of footprint area) use defaults; 2 of the 4 defaults are the overall-median fallback.
- **Threshold.** 5 OSM buildings fall between 0.3 and 0.7 (3 in 0.6-0.7, 1 in 0.3-0.4, 1 in 0.4-0.5); nothing between 0.5 and 0.6. The threshold is unchanged.
- 27 DBT buildings have no OSM attributes (unnamed in the UI).
- Two of the five tallest buildings carry no name (`dbt:EDIFC_4453`, `dbt:EDIFC_3004`); the tallest, Torre Velasca (100.18 m), is plausible but not checked against a cited source here.
