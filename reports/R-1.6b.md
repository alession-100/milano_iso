## Deviations and assumptions
- Interpretation of "union ... interior rings removed": I take the union of the rendered buildings first, then remove holes. My first attempt filled each building separately; palazzo_reale then measured 10.398 m (fail), because its courtyard is enclosed by several buildings (EDIFC_14168, 14192, 4565, 4651). Union-then-fill matches the ADR text and the orchestrator's 0.00 m. The tolerance is unchanged.
- The rendered-building set for the check requires `rendered = true`.
- Running `make data`/`make validate`/`make test` rewrote tracked artifacts under `reports/artifacts/T-1.*`; these are committed.
- I did not verify that the new heights test fails without the fix (pytest is not on the system Python here).

## Open questions and risks
- ADR-0018 should state that the fill applies to the union (courtyards enclosed by several buildings), not per building.
