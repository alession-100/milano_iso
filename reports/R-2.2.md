## Deviations and assumptions
1. **Buildings outside the AOI tiles.** 23 rendered buildings have a representative point outside the 4 AOI tiles (they fall in 8 neighbouring tiles: -2_-2, -2_-1, -2_0, -1_-2, -1_1, 0_1, 1_-1, 1_0; the DBT/OSM extraction extends beyond the AOI). Card AC1 (4 tiles) and AC5 (all rendered buildings in tiles) conflict. I kept AC1: by default only the 4 AOI tiles are written and the 23 are reported as excluded. `--include-outside` writes the 12-tile variant (flat layers are then also exported for those tiles, clipped to the tile box). I did not run the 12-tile variant for the final artifacts.
2. **Underground features skipped.** Roads, rail and other flat rows with `underground = true` (454 roads, 19 rail) are not drawn (the card is silent). Assumption: tunnels and building passages must not appear at ground level.
3. **Width validity.** A `width_raw` is accepted when it parses (optional trailing "m", comma decimal) and is in (0, 30] m; else the class width from `style.py`. The 30 m cap is a style choice. `road_areas` (polygons) have no ribbon.
4. **Feature table extras.** Roads and rail entries carry `osm_type`, `osm_id`, `class`, `width_m`, `width_source` (`osm` or `style`); water, green and landuse carry `osm_type`, `osm_id`, `kind`; road_areas `class`. No `name` (the layers have none). Floats rounded to 3 decimals.
5. **Layer heights** (style): landuse 0.02, green 0.04, water 0.06, road_areas 0.08, roads 0.12, rail 0.16 m. Widths per class in `style.py`; mitre limit 2.0.
6. **Mesh layout:** one glTF node, mesh and material per layer, named after the layer; bounds min/max on POSITION only. Cap triangulation uses `shapely.constrained_delaunay_triangles` (no Steiner points). Triangles of a polygon are sorted for determinism.
7. Overlapping ribbons (for example crossing roads) are not unioned; they overlap at the same height.
8. brotli not measured (module absent; not added).
9. No data was changed; `heights.py`, `normalise.py`, `docs/`, root Makefile untouched.

## Open questions and risks
- **Budget finding (escalation condition):** all four tiles exceed 250 KB gzip, by 1.5x to 2.6x (378,851 to 661,914 bytes). Not changed, as instructed. The size is dominated by flat-shaded duplicated vertices in `buildings` (for example 128,763 vertices in tile 0_0; each vertex costs 32 bytes: position 12, normal 12, feature id 4, index 4). Options for the orchestrator (ADR needed): quantised or indexed vertices, dropping the redundant uint32 index buffer for non-indexed meshes, smaller tiles, or a raised budget.
- Decide on Deviation 1: export neighbour tiles (12 tiles) or keep the 4 AOI tiles and drop 23 edge buildings, or assign edge buildings differently.
- Vertex colour/materials are default only, as specified.
