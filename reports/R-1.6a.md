## The palazzo_reale finding (escalation)
The reference (footprint centroid of the OSM way, 514954.44 / 5034395.59) is inside the OSM footprint (distance 0) but falls in a courtyard of the DBT footprint. The 4 DBT buildings carrying the id (all named Palazzo Reale) are: EDIFC_4565 (primary, 4950.3 m2, 22.26 m from the reference), EDIFC_14168 (1901.1 m2, 10.4 m), EDIFC_14192 (797.8 m2, 26.6 m), EDIFC_4651 (19.2 m2, 30.4 m). OSM area 7987.3 m2, union of the rendered four 7668.4 m2, intersection 6263.0 m2 (IoU 0.667). So the rule "reference within 5 m of the primary (largest-overlap) building" fails although the landmark is correctly assigned. Nothing was loosened. Options for the orchestrator: measure against the union of all rendered buildings carrying the id (the closest is still 10.4 m, so this also fails at 5 m), a different reference position for this entry, or an ADR-0017 amendment for landmarks that wrap courtyards.

## Deviations and assumptions
- Galleria: its OSM footprint is an unmatched `building=roof` (not rendered). Landmark-first over rendered buildings picks up `dbt:EDIFC_4893` as primary (IoU 0.006 with the OSM footprint, 18.3 m from the reference). The check keeps `deferred_phase4` when the landmark's own OSM building is not rendered, and records that incidental building key in the CSV. Consequence: the Galleria's `buildings` rows carry a landmark_id on an unrelated rendered building; the roof row has none. The card says a landmark that overlaps no rendered building keeps no assignment; here a (tiny) overlap exists, so it was assigned.
- Candidates for assignment are rendered buildings only (DBT buildings, and OSM-only buildings with `rendered` true).
- Registration pairing: each OSM building with its best-IoU DBT building (`buildings` source dbt, union of volumes); offset = DBT minus OSM centroid. The ADR's 41 pairs (orchestrator measurement) differ from my 48 because the pairing method is not specified; the magnitude agrees with the 0.22 m baseline. Sign convention is my choice; the magnitude does not depend on it.
- The CLI prints out-of-pilot landmarks under "no_osm_footprint" (9 listed, 6 of them are out of pilot or absent from `osm_buildings`); this is expected noise, not an error.
- `make data` also rewrote T-1.1/T-1.2/T-1.3 artifacts with CRLF-only differences; I reverted those, only T-1.4 and T-1.6 artifacts are committed.
