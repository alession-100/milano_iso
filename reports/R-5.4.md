## Deviations and assumptions
1. **"Boundary >= 3:1" is read as "border or fill" (and the focus indicator as "ring or halo").** A single colour cannot reach 3:1 against every colour of the real map: with a dark-grey border the measurement found 1.57:1 on a tan roof, and even the final extreme borders reach only 1.65:1 alone in day mode (`border alone` rows). WCAG 1.4.11 asks for 3:1 for the part of the component that identifies it, so I measure the better of border and fill per pixel, and chose extreme border colours so the two cover complementary lightness ranges. Recorded in ADR-0032. If the reviewer reads 4a item 3 as "border alone", day mode fails at 1.65 and the design would need an outer edge on every control.
2. **The focus colour in day mode is a dark navy (`#08306b`), not the `#0d47a1` first tried:** the first full measurement gave 2.94 over a mid-grey roof with the blue; the navy gives 3.58 (worst) with the white halos.
3. **Borders are extreme (day `#1c262b`, night `#ffffff`)**, replacing `#607d8b` and `#90a4ae`, for the reason in item 1. A visible style change of every control, not of the map.
4. **Attribution links and the card's OSM link are 44 px tall boxes.** This makes the attribution bar taller (44 px, or 2 lines at 412 px, about 100 px). I read "every target" literally, inline links included.
5. **Playwright safety-net timeouts raised** (`playwright.config.ts`: test 90 s, expect 20 s; a few polls 5 s or 10 s to 20 s; `debug.spec.ts` bench limits to 40 s and 60 s, test 150 s, and "within 20 s" removed from its title). Reason: the card's `--repeat-each=10` runs ten copies of each test at the same time on 10 workers with software GL; in my first two attempts 37 tests (existing and new) timed out at 30 s, among them tests that take 5 to 6 s alone, while the CPU was at 100% (chrome GPU processes at 400 to 780% each). These limits only stop a test that never settles; no test asserts a duration. This goes beyond the card's item 9 text, and is the only change to `playwright.config.ts`.
6. **Races found by my own repeat runs and fixed in my tests:** reading the card title before the index name replaced the tile name; pressing Enter before the search results existed (the index loads on the first input); two workers writing one snapshot file (now `testInfo.attach`). All three are the two causes named in the brief (reading state before it settled), now polled.
7. **`waitForTimeout` calls remain** in `controls.spec.ts` (gesture pacing and release rest), `toolbar.spec.ts`, `debug.spec.ts`, `smoke.spec.ts`, `tiles.spec.ts` and `rotation.spec.ts`. They are pauses (before checking for stray console messages, or between synthetic pointer events), not assertions on durations, and I left them: removing them is outside this card and touches gesture tests. Every spec that samples render or tile state now uses the shared helpers.
8. **The `?debug` overlay's own strings** are re-labelled on a language change but it is a developer tool, kept out of the touch-target scan. Its two numeric lines are on the i18n allow-list as technical output.
9. **"Languages" of the attribution:** only the label "Dati/Data" is translated; the attribution entries come from the manifest and stay as data.
10. **Fixture vs real data:** the contrast, screenshots and snapshots are from real tiles; the layout, touch and keyboard tests run on the fixture (their UI code paths are identical). The notice in the real-data measurement comes from aborting one tile request in the browser, not from a real failure.
11. **Assumption:** the minimap on desktop is a pointer-only affordance (the keyboard alternative is search, as its label says). It is not in the tab order.
12. **Search status "7 risultati" stays in the (screen-reader-only) status region after a result is chosen**; existing behaviour, not changed.
13. **Draw-call "unchanged" is shown against the G4 numbers (140/146)**, not by running the old build again: the scene, tile and style code are untouched, and the language switch is tested not to change the count.
