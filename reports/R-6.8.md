ADR-0043 rule (`memory_60min.json`, `analysis`): coverage ok (all 50 zoom phases, widths 250 to 3,999.8 m); heap adjusted, heap raw, external, GPU process all pass; geometries within the cache bound (max 148, bound 391), textures 0 orphans, tiles in scene 73 against max(64, peakWanted 78), DOM constant, programs 6 to 7 after warm-up; **renderer process fails**: first-judged median 361.4 MB, last-minute median 541.2 MB (+49.7 %, limit 20 %), slope 3.29 MB/min (limit 6 in the rule over the judged window; the card's limit is 1 MB/min over 40-60). Overall `pass: false`, exit 1.

**Plateau verdict: no plateau.** The renderer rises linearly, about 3.3 MB/min in every window from minute 4 to 60, from 350 MB to 540 MB. The 0.5 MB/min plateau test over the last 20 minutes fails by a factor of about 6.6. The R-6.7 reading that growth "levels off after minute 3 to 4" holds only in the sense that the early 80 MB/min warm-up ended; a steady 3 to 4 MB/min remains (R-6.7 saw 2 to 3 MB/min at 20 minutes). The JS heap, external memory and the GPU process are flat, so the growth is in memory the JS heap does not count: native renderer-process memory (Blink/V8 off-heap, image decode, ANGLE/WebGL client-side buffers) or allocator fragmentation. Shards loaded grew from 8 to 86 and stopped at 86 from minute 40 on, while the renderer kept rising at the same rate, so the creep is not explained by the shard catalogue.

## Open questions and risks
- **Decision for the orchestrator (escalation):** renderer memory grows about 3.3 MB/min indefinitely (about 200 MB per hour). A user who leaves the page open for several hours at the default view could pass 1 GB in the renderer. Options: a task that adds a tracing hook to `memory-session.mjs` (a `Tracing` with `disabled-by-default-memory-infra` dump at minute 10 and at the end) and compares allocator categories; or a bisect over the closed-path 15-minute run of T-6.7 to isolate which phase (zoom, drag, wheel, tile eviction) drives it; or accept it with a documented limit.
- 1920x1080 on this machine is 90 to 93 fps; the 95 fps target from the card is not met by either main or 5a9d237 today, so the earlier 101 fps was a better-than-now machine state.
