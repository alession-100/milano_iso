## Design choices (values documented in code)
- Camera at target + direction x 20 km, near 10 km, far 30 km. Reasoning (in `VIEW_DEFAULTS`): at max zoom (4000 m) on a tall phone viewport the ground spans about +-7.5 km in depth, plus 350 m buildings, inside the +-10 km window. Depth resolution about 1.2 mm.
- Screen mapping: right = (cos az, -sin az), away = (-sin az, -cos az) in (x, z); ground along "away" appears sin(el) on screen.
- Pointer handling: one generic gesture (centroid and finger distance) covers one-finger pan, two-finger pan and pinch.
- Inertia: screen-space velocity (px/s) from samples in the last 100 ms, exponential decay tau 250 ms, exact integral per step (frame-rate independent), stop below 0.5% of the viewport width per second. Runs on rAF only while active.
- Keyboard pan: 10% of the visible width as a **ground distance** along the screen-aligned ground axes (so vertical arrows move the picture by 10% x sin(el) of the width on screen). The card does not say which; flagged.
- Wheel: factor = exp(-deltaY * 0.0015), line/page delta modes scaled.

## Deviations and assumptions
- **ADR-0012 console filter widened by one optional suffix (needs an orchestrator/ADR decision).** The e2e filter in `controls.spec.ts` is the smoke regex plus `(?: \(this message will no longer repeat\))?`. Reason: with many rendered frames (drags, wheel), without any screenshot, headless Chromium emits the same SwiftShader "GPU stall due to ReadPixels" message for the last occurrence with that suffix, and the unmodified regex fails 100% of such runs (reproduced 6/6 on the drag and wheel tests). The message text is otherwise identical. `smoke.spec.ts` was not changed. If you decline the widening, an ADR amendment or another approach is needed. I did not alter smoke's regex.
- Zoom is instant in all modes (no animated zoom), so reduced motion needs no zoom special case. The card only requires instant zoom under reduced motion.
- `main.ts`: the three import lines are outside the delimited block. `requestRender()` is `export`ed from `main.ts` as the card asks; because `main.ts` is the entry module nothing imports it yet.
- Existing `render()` still runs once directly at startup and on `resize`; it was not changed. `requestRender()` coalesces everything else through one rAF. There is no permanent loop.
- Bounds default (+-500 m) is a viewer constant; T-3.2 is to call `rig.setBounds(...)`. The rig is not exported from `main.ts`; T-3.2 will need access (for example via the main.ts block) - orchestrator to wire.
- Style constants (zoom 60-4000, 600 m, tau 250 ms, key steps, wheel sensitivity) are labelled "style/UX constants" in `VIEW_DEFAULTS`.
- Hello-world scene is 10 m wide, so the screenshot needs zoom to near the minimum of 60 m to show it.
- `i18n.ts` modified although not listed in Outputs.

## Open questions and risks
- The headless run exercises touch through CDP; real-device touch (iOS Safari, inertial scrolling by the browser) is not tested.
- Clamping at bounds during a zoom may shift the point under the cursor slightly (documented limitation; the within-bounds invariant is tested).
- `npm audit` still reports the 7 moderate dev-dependency findings from ADR-0012 (unchanged).
