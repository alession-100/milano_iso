## Day-look changes (before/after, same view)
Before shots (code at `f42d7fa`): `reports/artifacts/T-4.3/before/before-none-{default,close}-*.png`. After: `day-default-*.png` and `after-day-close-*.png` (same camera: default, and 150 m at x 0, z 40).
1. **Toggle button** top right, visible in every day screenshot (new UI, not a map change).
2. **Green by kind:** `park` and `garden` keep palette v1 green `0xa9c58f` exactly. `grass`, `meadow` and similar get the new lighter `0xbfd49b`; wood-like kinds darker `0x8caf7c`; pitch and playground `0x9fc3a2`. In the real pilot data the green kinds are `park`, `garden`, `grass`, `flowerbed` (counted from the tile tables), so only `grass` patches look different in day (compare the green patch at the lower right of the close view, before and after).
3. **Land-use by kind:** `residential`, `commercial` get slightly different greys (`0xd9d1c0`, `0xddcec3`) close to v1 `0xd5d0c1`; `construction` and unknown kinds keep v1. The change is barely visible because buildings cover most of it.
4. **Water:** `reflecting_pool` gets a lighter blue `0x9cc7d9`, other water the v1 colour, plus a static sheen (about +-5% brightness bands). Pilot data has 2 `water` and 3 `reflecting_pool` features, so it shows only in small basins.
5. Lighting: no change to day lights or shadows. Day palette v1 entries are unchanged (a unit test pins their order and key values).

## Deviations and assumptions
- Night lit windows (optional in the card) not done.
- Water look is the sheen only (no edge band; there is no per-vertex distance data in the tiles, and adding one is a pipeline change).
- The kind groups are my own style mapping: the kinds come from `classify.py` and the real tile tables, the colours are style, not data.
- Night palette values and light intensities were tuned by eye on the real data and are candidates for the owner's approval.
- `web/e2e/fixtures` have no landuse, green or water rows, so the kind colours are covered by unit tests and real-data screenshots, not by the fixture baselines.
- I put the toggle at the top right. The page background and ground follow the mode (`documentElement` background and the scene background).
- The screenshots were taken with a throwaway script kept outside the repo (software GL, `?e2e=1`); the console message counts in `shots-log.txt` for day runs are the ADR-0012 ReadPixels noise, which the e2e filter covers.
- The night-mode canvas is not antialiased in e2e only (ADR-0027); the same as day.
