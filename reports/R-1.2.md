## Deviations and assumptions
1. **No single `EDIFC` Shapefile exists in the ZIP.** The class is split into `EDIFC_CR_EDF_IS` (ground footprint, Polygon Z, 33,744), `EDIFC_CR_EDF_ME` (maximum extent, Polygon, 33,893) and `EDIFC_CR_EDF_ME_SR` (sub-areas with `CR_EDF_POR`, 61,167). The card asks for layers `edifc` and `un_vol`. I wrote `edifc` = `EDIFC_CR_EDF_IS` and added `edifc_me` and `edifc_me_sr`. Meanings come from spec p.142 (CR_EDF spatial components).
2. **Spec PDF URL.** DATA_SOURCES row 3a gives the filename but not the URL. I took the link from the T-0.3 artefact `reports/artifacts/T-0.3/dbt_specifiche_page.html` (outside the card's input list, read only for this href). Size matches 12,386,999.
3. **Page numbers** are the PDF's printed footers ("pag.N di 456"), which equal the page index (checked on pp.129-135).
4. **Sort keys:** `CLASSREF` (edifc, edifc_me), `SUBREGID` (edifc_me_sr), `CLASSID` (un_vol), ordered by text prefix then integer suffix (natural order). All unique.
5. **Intersection:** the extent polygon (EPSG:32632 bbox, edges densified to 10 m, transformed to EPSG:7791) is used as a bbox prefilter, then an exact 2D `intersects`; features are kept whole.
6. The `canonical dump` hashes layer name, CRS, geometry type, column names/dtypes, then per row WKB (3D, little-endian) and `repr` of attributes, in stored order. It ignores gpkg file metadata.
7. Layers reported as "X Z" in `schema.md` are GDAL "Measured 3D X" layers (pyogrio converts); the M values are not examined.
8. Files written by Python on Windows (`manifests`, `summary.json`, `schema.md`) use CRLF line endings; git warns it will normalise to LF. Contents are otherwise deterministic. Not changed, to stay in scope.
9. Units of `UN_VOL_AV` are not stated in the spec; "metres" is an inference (height_semantics.md point 3).
10. `retrieved_utc` in the manifests is the time of the first download in this worktree run (2026-11-15).

## Open questions and risks
- **Decision for orchestrator (T-1.3/T-1.4):** 47 of 4,657 pilot volumes (1.0%) belong to `EDI_MIN_*` (edificio minore, spec p.151, also a subclass of CR_EDF), not `EDIFC`. Citywide 13.49% of volumes do. 100% of `CEDIUV` values resolve to `EDIFC_CR_EDF_ME` plus `EDI_MIN_CR_EDF_ME`. The card scoped only EDIFC and UN_VOL, so `EDI_MIN` is not extracted. Decide whether a later task extracts it.
- `UN_VOL_POR` 03 (portico ceiling): `QE - baseZ - AV` is a constant 3.5 for all 212 pilot records; cause not in the spec. Heights for these volumes need care.
- Zero heights (287) and two -0.001 values exist citywide but not in the pilot; meaning not in spec. Matters for Phase 6.
- The vertical datum of `UN_VOL_QE` is not in the spec.
- CRS: pyproj selects one operation, accuracy 1.0 m, no datum shift (only GRS80 to WGS84 ellipsoid); computed shift at the pilot centre about 1e-4 m.
- The EPSG:7791 to 32632 relation was not tested with real control points; the positional claim comes from the PROJ definition only.
- Escalation conditions checked: URLs worked, sizes matched, `UN_VOL` and the EDIFC layers exist, the join is established, and the spec does not contradict above-ground heights.
