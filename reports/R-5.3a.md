## Design as built
- **Names:** roads/green/water in `processed/pilot.gpkg` have no `name` column. The names come from the `named_features` layer by exact `(osm_type, osm_id)` join (no normalise change; 667 of 2308 roads match). So the card's "road names not available" escalation did not apply.
- **Entry fields:** `name`, `kind`, `centre`, `bbox` ([xmin, zmin, xmax, zmax], x east, z = -(N-N0), 0.1 m), `tiles`, `ref`. `ref` per kind: building `{"key"}`; street `{"osm_ids": {"w": [...], "r": [...]}}` (one street can mix ways and relations, e.g. Piazza San Babila); green/water `{"osm_type","osm_id"}`; landmark `{"landmark_id"}`.
- **centre:** area centroid of the rendered pieces; for streets the centroid of the piece nearest to the bbox centre, so it lies on the street.
- **bbox/tiles of streets, green, water:** from the rendered (clipped to tile and extent) pieces; `tiles` are the tiles where a table row exists.
- **Landmarks:** a building with `landmark_id` produces only the landmark entry (name from `tests/landmarks.yaml`), never a building entry, so "Palazzo Carminati" is not listed (the building carrying it is the Generali landmark). The Galleria has no rendered building (ADR-0016), so its entry comes from the OSM roof footprint used by its detail spec; its `tiles` are the tiles holding its details.
- **Minimap:** layers green, water, minor roads (living_street, residential, unclassified), major roads (motorway to tertiary), buildings, with a ground rect. `<style>` holds the default day colours (from `web/src/style/palette.ts`) as CSS classes `mm-*`; there are no `fill`/`stroke` attributes. Stroke widths 4 and 9 m are style, not data. Tolerance 1.0 m (Douglas-Peucker), then snap to 0.1 m integer coordinates, canonical ring start and orientation, sorted paths, relative steps.

## Deviations and assumptions
1. **No polygon union for buildings** in the minimap. The card says "building union". A GEOS union is one of the steps that differs across platforms (CLAUDE.md lesson). Footprints are drawn as separate sub-paths in one path with exterior/hole orientation, so the default non-zero fill rule gives the same picture. The result is not a merged outline; file size is as measured above.
2. **Roads in the minimap** are the original centrelines (not the tile ribbons), drawn for motorway/trunk/primary/secondary/tertiary ("major") and living_street/residential/unclassified ("minor"). Footways, pedestrian, service and rail are not drawn (assumption: "major roads" plus the ordinary streets, so that the centre is readable).
3. **Street `ref`** is `{"osm_ids": {"w": [...], "r": [...]}}`, not a flat `osm_type` + `osm_ids`, because real data has streets mixing ways and relations ("Piazza San Babila"). Clients must read both keys.
4. **Landmark entry without a rendered building (Galleria)**: extent from the roof footprint (the OSM `building=roof` the detail spec uses); `tiles` are only the tiles with its details (one tile in the pilot), not every tile the roof touches.
5. **Index scope**: roads (not `road_areas`), green and water only; landuse, rail and named points (shops, amenities) are not indexed, per the card.
6. Fixture names (Via Fixture, Parco Fixture, Fixture Landmark) are invented, fixture only. They do not change the fixture's `.glb` or feature `.json` files (verified: only `tiles.json`, `index.json`, `minimap.svg` changed).
7. `web/src/style/palette.test.ts` and `trees.test.ts` were edited (file filter), outside the card's literal file list, because the new `index.json` in the fixture directory broke them. Minimal change.
8. `index.json` also has `format_version`, `frame` and `kinds` header fields (not in the card), for the client.
9. `make test`/`make validate`/the export rewrite tracked files in `reports/artifacts/T-2.3` and `T-4.1` (the export summary's marking vertex counts differ slightly from the committed copy, e.g. `-2_-2 markings` 974 vs 972 vertices; this existed before this task). I reverted both and did not commit them. The `T-1.6` summary is committed (it lists the new data tests).
10. The first `make data` on the copied cache re-ran the full data pipeline (including osm_extract); that is the card's "run `make data` once".

## Open questions and risks
- Linux/CI was not run. Index values are rounded to 0.1 m and the minimap is integer arithmetic after one `simplify`, and a +-1e-9 m shift test passes for both, but a knife-edge case (a coordinate exactly on a rounding boundary) could still differ between GEOS builds; I could not check that here. The orchestrator should confirm on CI.
- Whole-city minimap size (about 5.4 MB gzip at the current settings) must be redesigned in Phase 6 (see estimate).

- The client does not yet fetch or show `index.json` / `minimap.svg`; that is T-5.1 / T-5.3b.
