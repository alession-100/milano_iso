## The layout rule (B1, N1, N1b)
Two layouts, chosen by size only (no per-size pixel offsets):
- **Roomy** (width >= 700 and height >= 600): minimap fixed bottom-left in its own column; the attribution's `max-width` is `100vw - 12 - 172`, so it wraps inside the rest of the width and never goes under the minimap. Info card is a panel at the right; every panel/notice of the toolbar zone is at most 360 px wide, so it ends before the card (700 - 306 > 6 + 360).
- **Otherwise** (width < 700, or height < 600): the minimap is behind its button, in the toolbar zone flow; the info card is the bottom sheet (width < 700) above the attribution; the zone leaves room for the right-hand buttons (width < 700).
- While the layers or help panel is open, the tiles notice is hidden (it comes back when the panel closes). Without this, a column of toolbar + layers (266 px) + notice + card + attribution does not fit at 915x412 or 360x640.

### Landmark rule (N2)
From the feature table, `buildings` rows with the landmark's `landmark_id` (DBT volumes; stylised `landmark_details` are never read). Shown only if: there are such rows; every tile listed for the landmark in `index.json` (`tiles`) is loaded; all rows state the same `height_m` and `height_source`. The OSM link is shown only if all rows share one `osm:<w|n|r><id>` key. Otherwise the field is simply absent (no placeholder).
Real data (`web/public/tiles/pilot`, read from `data_t53a`): Duomo 1 row, 94.579 m dbt -> shown as 94,6 m; Torre Velasca 1 row, 100.179 m; Teatro alla Scala 1 row, 38.312 m; Palazzo Marino 1 row, 26.807 m; Palazzo delle Assicurazioni Generali 1 row, 29.878 m. **Palazzo Reale: 4 body rows with different heights (22.052, 25.678, 19.412, 19.397 m) -> no height is shown.** No real landmark body has an `osm:` key (all `dbt:EDIFC_*`), so no real landmark card shows an OSM link; the OSM ids of `tests/landmarks.yaml` are not in the tiles or in `index.json`, and I did not add them (card scope: web only, no pipeline).
