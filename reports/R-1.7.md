## Deviations and assumptions
- ADR-0020 implemented literally. Chosen details where it is silent: R = union of all rendered footprints (layer `buildings`, `rendered = true`) with distance <= 60 m to F, then exteriors only (courtyards filled); coarse grid 2 m over +/-50 m; fine grid 0.25 m over +/-2 m around the chosen coarse cell; near-maximal means >= 99.5% of the maximum; ties/near-ties resolved to the smallest |t| (coarse first, then refined); `plateau_width_m` = the largest distance between near-maximal coarse-grid translations (0.0 means a single coarse cell). No overlap at all within the window gives `fail_no_overlap`.
- The search function lives in `tests/data/landmark_offset.py` (card allows `tests/data/`); the unit test in `tests/pipeline/` adds `tests/data` to `sys.path`.
- `ground_neighbours` is a new column (card asks to "record the neighbour count" without naming a column); `dbt_volumes` and `render_volumes` carry it, OSM rows have NA.
- "Neighbour" = POR 01 volumes of any building (the building itself has none); distance is measured to the union of the building's volumes (its footprint as used by heights).
- Same-geometry duplicates in `normalise.py` are logged (`flagged`), not dropped, since none occur outside the exempt layer.
- The `ME` footprint is read from the existing `edifc_me` / `edi_min_me` layers of `dbt_pilot.gpkg`; no change to `dbt_extract` behaviour.
- "-0.0" normalised in the float columns of `dbt_volumes`, `render_volumes`, `buildings` and in the formatted CSV/MD numbers; I did not find any `-0.0` in the committed artifacts afterwards (grep).
- `newline="\n"` applied to all text/CSV/JSON writers in `pipeline/milano_iso` outside tiles (`dbt_extract.py`, `fetch.py`, `heights.py`, `normalise.py`, `osm_extract.py`) and in `tests/data`. `fetch.py` and `osm_extract.py` are not named in the card's edit list; the change is limited to that argument.
- `ruff check .` (whole repo) also reports 2 pre-existing issues in `reports/artifacts/T-0.3/dbt_dbf_probe.py`; I reverted the auto-fix I had triggered there. `make lint` (pipeline, tests) is clean.
- ADR-0015 and ADR-0020 texts not edited (orchestrator's job).
