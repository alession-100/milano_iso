## Deviations and assumptions
- Search details where ADR-0020 is silent: coarse grid 2 m over +/-50 m; fine grid 0.25 m over +/-2 m around the chosen coarse cell; near-maximal = >= 99.5% of the maximum; smallest |t| wins, applied on the coarse grid first and then on the fine grid; `plateau_width_m` = largest distance between near-maximal coarse-grid translations (0.0 = single coarse cell); no overlap in the window gives `fail_no_overlap`.
- `best_offset` lives in `tests/data/landmark_offset.py`; the unit test adds `tests/data` to `sys.path`.
- `ground_neighbours` is a new column (card did not name one). "Neighbour" = POR 01 volumes of any other building; distance to the union of the building's volumes.
- Same-geometry duplicates are logged (`flagged`), not dropped; none occur outside the exempt layer.
- `-0.0` normalised in float columns of `dbt_volumes`, `render_volumes`, `buildings` and in formatted CSV/MD numbers; none found in the committed artifacts.
- `newline="\n"` also applied in `fetch.py` and `osm_extract.py` (not named in the card; argument only).
- `make tiles` run once so the merged T-2.2 data tests could run; it writes only under `data/tiles` (gitignored).
- Repo-wide `ruff check .` has 2 pre-existing findings in `reports/artifacts/T-0.3/dbt_dbf_probe.py` (untouched); `make lint` is clean.
