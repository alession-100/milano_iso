## What changed
- `web/src/main.ts`: resolved. One on-demand render model: `requestRender()` coalesces into one `requestAnimationFrame(render)`; no permanent loop. `render()` runs `updateTiles()` only if the camera key changed since the last cull, then renders and records `renderer.info` into the `render` test state. Controls (and their inertia frames), resize, every tile load/evict (`onLoaded`, `onEvict`, `onChange`) and the manifest load call `requestRender()`. After the manifest loads: `rig.setBounds(contentBounds(manifest))`.
- `web/src/i18n.ts`: resolved. Keys `mapLabel`, `dataLabel`, `tilesError` in `it` and `en`; the `attribution` placeholder is gone. Parity test unchanged and passing.
- `web/src/tiles/tempview.ts`: deleted, with `fitCamera`, `?view` and its `parseView` unit test.
- `web/src/testhook.ts`: `registerSetView()` exposes `window.__milanoIsoSetView(x, z, width)` only when `e2e=1` (calls the rig).
- `web/src/tiles/culling.test.ts`: builds its test cameras with `createCamera` + `CameraRig` instead of `applyView`.
- `web/e2e/controls.spec.ts`: uses the shared `collectProblems` from `support.ts` (own copy removed); now serves the fixture tiles and waits for the manifest bounds; the clamp test also asserts the bounds equal the fixture content rectangle.
- `web/e2e/tiles.spec.ts`: uses `__milanoIsoSetView` instead of `?view=`.
- `web/e2e/support.ts`: new `fixtureContentBounds()` (computed independently from the fixture JSON).
- `reports/artifacts/T-3.5/`: 3 screenshots and `real_measurements.json`.

## Deviations and assumptions
1. The task said "create branch from current HEAD", but that HEAD (42aac3c) predated T-3.1 and the card; the card says to cut from `main`. I reset the new `t-3.5` to `main` before merging `t-3.2`. No work was lost.
2. The bounds test is an extension of the existing clamp test (asserting manifest bounds, not the 500 m fallback) rather than a separate test. Because bounds now come from the manifest, `controls.spec.ts` serves the fixture tiles; without them the failed manifest fetch logs a console error.
3. Real-data measurements used a throwaway Playwright script kept outside the repo (scratchpad), on port 4174 to avoid the e2e port; the preview was stopped afterwards.
4. The `SCREENSHOT_NOISE` suffix widening of ADR-0012 (from R-3.1/R-3.2) is unchanged; the ADR-0012 amendment is the orchestrator's.
5. `render()` calls `camera.updateMatrixWorld()` before computing the camera key, since three.js only updates it lazily inside `renderer.render`.
