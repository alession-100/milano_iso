## Item 2 - 50 random buildings

### Seed and sampling (`scripts/s2_sample.py`)
- **Frame:** the 67,774 rendered rows of `buildings` in `processed/city.gpkg`, sorted by `building_key`.
- **District:** the Municipio (1-9) that contains each building's `representative_point()`.
  - Boundaries come from Comune di Milano open data ds379, `ds379_municipi_label.geojson` (CC BY), retrieved 2026-11-24 07:13 UTC: 3,447,281 B, sha256 `913e39d7c5970e9053c68c07c50cbcda3f154f95904f447fe2ef08b115264581`. It was used only for this audit.
  - 168 rendered buildings fall outside every Municipio polygon, and are outside the frame.
- **Strata by height source:** `dbt` 20, `default` 10, `osm_height` 10, `osm_levels` 10. This is deliberately not proportional; the population is 64,942 / 2,378 / 179 / 275.
- **Spread over districts:** within each source, the draws go round-robin over the Municipi, starting at 1 (dbt), 3 (default), 5 (osm_height) and 7 (osm_levels), and skipping empty cells.
- **Draw:**
  - `numpy.random.default_rng(20261124)`, **seed 20261124**, one generator.
  - Cells are visited in source order (alphabetical), then by Municipio ascending.
  - Each cell is sorted by `building_key` and drawn with `rng.choice(len(pool), k, replace=False)`.
  - The result is sorted by `building_key`.
  - Cell sizes and chosen indices are in `sample50.csv`.

### Method per building
- **Source geometry:**
  - `dbt:` buildings: the union of the raw `UN_VOL` polygons with that `CEDIUV`, read from `DBT_2020.zip` and reprojected. That is the pipeline's footprint source; the EDIFC outline was not compared.
  - `osm:` buildings: the raw PBF element.
- **Rendered footprint:** the union of the horizontal (cap) triangles of the building's feature id in its base-tile `.glb`.
- **Region Hausdorff:** both footprints treated as point sets, on a 1 cm grid. The boundary-vertex Hausdorff is reported too; see the note below the table.
- **Height rules, recomputed from raw:**
  - **DBT:**
    - top = `UN_VOL_QE` - ground, where ground is the lowest exterior Z of the building's POR 01 volumes;
    - `UN_VOL_AV <= 0` gives the default;
    - the building's height is the maximum top.
  - **OSM:**
    - the height is `height` parsed (`m`, `ft`), else `building:levels` x 3, else the default;
    - the base is `min_height`, else `building:min_level` x 3.
  - **Default:** 2.817 m (`reports/artifacts/T-6.1/city/T-1.4/defaults.json`).

- **tile:** the base tile, with the number of times the key occurs there. The expected tile, from the representative point, matched in 50 of 50.

## Risks
- **R1. ADR-0020 fails on Pirelli at 22.18 m.**
  - The rendered tower is correctly placed (0.95 m, IoU 0.947); the metric fails on the podium plateau.
  - The suite never runs ADR-0020 on the eight non-pilot landmarks (`_entries()` keeps `in_pilot` only), so extending it to the city as written would fail.
  - A metric that cannot tell a wide flat overlap from a misplacement needs an owner decision, for example comparing against the landmark's tall volumes, or capping the plateau.
- **R2. Torre Libeskind/PwC (`citylife_pwc`) is drawn 2.82 m tall, against 175 m in OSM (`height=175`).**
  - The DBT 2020 survey has it "in costruzione" with `UN_VOL_AV = 0`, so ADR-0036 gives it the 2.817 m default, and DBT-first matching ignores the OSM height.
  - The position is fine, but the landmark is effectively missing in 3D.
  - The same rule leaves 5 more DBT buildings at about 3 m whose OSM element has 7 to 18 levels: EDIFC_16056 (12), 16063 (14), 16064 "Torre Aurora" (18), 16065 (7), 16081 "Ospedale Galeazzi" (16).
  - Of 214 DBT-source defaults, 107 have a matched OSM element; 1 has an OSM `height` and 11 have `building:levels`.
  - This follows the accepted rules, but it is a visible accuracy defect, and the fallback order is an owner decision.
- **R3. The attribution does not name the Comune boundary dataset (row 4a, CC BY) on its own.**
  - That dataset defines the drawn extent. The "Comune di Milano" credit and the CC BY link may be enough, but no document says so.
  - CC BY 4.0 also asks for modifications to be indicated (3(a)(1)(B)), and the UI does not do that.
  - In English mode the DBT credit stays in Italian.
  - T-7.2 (licence table, About page) is the place to settle this.

## Non-blocking issues
- **N1. `stadio_meazza` is unresolved** (see above). The stadium is drawn from 44 OSM parts, 3.41 m from its outline centroid; 33 roof parts are not rendered, and no landmark id is attached.
- **N2. Cracks between adjacent DBT volumes.** Each volume is simplified separately, so adjacent volumes can open small gaps.
  - In the sample, 3 of 21 DBT buildings have them:
    - EDIFC_15147: 38 gaps, 3.03 m2 in total, widest 0.294 m;
    - EDIFC_5371: 3 gaps, widest 0.030 m;
    - EDIFC_24533: 1 gap of 0.005 m.
  - Each side stays within 0.2 m, so the tolerance is met.
  - A gap of about 0.3 m may show as a slit at the closest zoom, about 0.12 m per pixel at 150 m wide on 1280 px. Not checked visually.
- **N3.** The feature name "Piazza Alvar Aalto" on `dbt:EDIFC_6461` (150.09 m, the 4th tallest) comes from the matched OSM element, and looks like a square's name on a tower. Not investigated.

## What could not be verified
- **Linux:** byte identity and behaviour on Linux were not checked; this audit ran on Windows only and makes no claim about CI.
- **CTBUH:** skyscrapercenter.com was not fetched; its figure comes through Wikipedia's citation.
- **OSM snapshot:**
  - The OSM source for the 50 buildings is the same PBF snapshot the pipeline used, re-read independently.
  - The 16 landmarks were also checked against the live API (2026-11-24, versions unchanged); the 29 sampled OSM buildings were not.
- **DBT outlines:** DBT buildings were compared with the union of their raw `UN_VOL` volumes, not with the EDIFC outline layer.
- **Neighbour-median ground:** all 40 recomputed DBT buildings had their own POR 01 ground, so this branch of the height rule was not exercised.
- **Sampling frame:** the 168 rendered buildings outside the ds379 Municipi polygons were excluded from the draw.
- **Rendering:** attribution was checked in software GL (swiftshader) only, on the HEAD build. Its only source difference from `t66data/dist1` is a test hook in `main.ts`.
