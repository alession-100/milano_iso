## Deviations and assumptions
- `maxDiffPixelRatio` lowered from 0.01 to 0.001: at 0.01 the sabotages for "other", "landmark" and the pedestrian road passed (small objects), even after moving them into the close view. Noise measured 0 differing pixels over 30 + 40 captures on this machine. Linux noise is unmeasured (CI baselines come later); if CI flakes, ratio may need raising at the cost of small-object sensitivity.
- Fixture buildings B4, B5, B6 moved and one footway added (geometry is synthetic).
- Playwright does not overwrite an existing baseline that is within threshold, so old PNGs were deleted before regenerating (noted in the README).
- `visual-baselines.yml` sets `CI: ""` for the update step so the existing CI skip does not skip generation. Its `on` key is quoted (`"on":`) because PyYAML parses bare `on` as True and the card's verification command (`d['on']`) would raise KeyError; GitHub accepts both. `ci.yml` is unchanged.
- The Python side of ci.yml is not needed in the new workflow, so uv/Python are omitted.
