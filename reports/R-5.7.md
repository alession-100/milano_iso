## Deviations and assumptions
- **Real tiles** were read from `agent-a6736ef53cb2c6ee2/data_t56` via `make web-tiles` for the screenshots only; I removed `web/public/tiles/pilot` and rebuilt before the e2e runs, so the e2e ran on the build without real tiles. No pipeline run, no tracked evidence of earlier tasks changed.
- **Numbers chosen (style constants, not data):** the card keeps at least 64 px when it shares the area above the band with the toolbar zone (title and close button); the compact minimap scales between 80 and 200 px wide; a scrolling panel never goes below one 44 px row; 6 px button padding at widths 360-399 px. The first attempt with a 96 px card floor and 96 px minimum failed the sweep at 360 px wide, because the toolbar needed three rows there (144 px); the two-row toolbar and the lower floors are what made 360x360 fit. Recorded in ADR-0034.
- **Zone served first:** when the card is a bottom sheet and a panel is open on a short screen, the panel gets its natural height first and the card shrinks (and scrolls inside itself) down to 64 px. At 360x360 with the card, a panel and no notice, the card is 64 px tall; nothing the user needs is hidden (the toolbar, all panels reachable by scrolling, the card's title and close button visible). So the escalation condition did not occur.
- **The notice yields before a panel scrolls** (whenever the zone would be short, with or without the card). Where it yields it is a 1 px clipped box inside the live region with its close button `visibility: hidden`; it is excluded from the overlap checks in the sweep (nothing is drawn).
- **Desktop minimap moved up** to stand on the band (was bottom 6 px, beside the attribution). ADR-0034 records it; `toolbar.spec.ts` checks it.
- **The attribution** is right-aligned as before and may now use the whole width (no column left for the minimap), which gives a shorter band on desktop (56 px at 1280x720).
- **Sweep mutation proof** left out the lines about `--band` (T-5.5 has no such variable); a sweep with that assertion would fail trivially there.
- **Tab order change:** the notice's close button is now a Tab stop while a panel is open (it is drawn there when there is room); `ui.spec.ts` expects it.
- The sweep wait is 4 animation frames after a state change (no wall-clock assertion); `setViewportSize` is used to walk the widths, so the app sees real `resize` events.
- `*-linux.png` baselines untouched; the Linux toolbar baselines do not exist (toolbar baselines are win32 only, as before).
- No test asserts a duration. The ADR text was written before the numbers were final; the 64/80 values in it match the code.
