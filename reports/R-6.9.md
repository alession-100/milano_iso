# R-6.9 - What makes renderer-process memory grow

- **Status:** PARTIAL - escalation, outcome (b). The growth is not an app allocation: it exists only when the Playwright driver is attached. The card's "Escalate if" condition 1 applies (growing category inside Chrome or the driver, no app change affects it). No app fix was made; ADR-0044 is **proposed** in `docs/DECISIONS.md` and nothing in the rule was loosened.
- **Branch / commits:** `t-6.9` from main ca0b020.

## What changed
- `web/scripts/memory-session.mjs`: `--dumps 600,2400` (memory-infra dump through CDP `Tracing`, category `disabled-by-default-memory-infra`, `Tracing.requestMemoryDump` detailed; summary of the renderer's allocators to `<out>.dump<s>.json`, raw trace gzipped into `--dump-dir`), `--dump-dir`, `--motion full|path|zoom|pan|wheel|drag|still`. Default behaviour (`full`, no dumps) unchanged.
- `web/scripts/memory-dump-compare.mjs` (new): compares two dump summaries, prints allocator categories and changes in MB.
- `web/scripts/memory-nodriver.mjs` (new): the same closed camera path with no automation driver (Chrome started by the script, raw CDP only for `Target.createTarget`, `Runtime.evaluate`, browser-level `SystemInfo` and `Tracing`). Windows sampler.
- `docs/DECISIONS.md` ADR-0044 (proposed), `docs/PROGRESS.md` T-6.9 row, `reports/artifacts/T-6.9/`.
- No app (`web/src`), pipeline, dependency, baseline or threshold change. Raw traces (8 to 10 MB each) stayed outside the repo.

## Setup
Own `npm ci` and `vite build` in `web/`, tiles copied from `t66data/dist1/tiles` (read-only). Served on 4213 (my process, killed at the end). Headed Chrome (channel chrome), one run at a time. 4173 and 4174 untouched. Metrics are the page's `private_footprint_bytes` from memory-infra (the renderer with a `webgl` allocator) and the OS renderer sum used by the session (all renderer processes, including two small helper renderers of about 60 MB together).

## Evidence

### 1. What grows (40-minute Playwright run, default path, dumps at minute 10 and 40)
`reports/artifacts/T-6.9/memory_40min.json`, `dump_compare_600_vs_2400.md`. Page renderer private footprint 322.4 to 431.8 MB (+109.4 MB over 30 min). Rule slope 3.66 MB/min (OS), the rule fails on the renderer as in R-6.8; heap adjusted -0.04, external +0.005 MB/min.

| allocator | min 10 | min 40 | change MB | MB/min |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| blink_gc (Blink Oilpan heap, `main/heap/NormalPageSpace3` 28.4 to 110.6) | 31.9 | 114.8 | +82.9 | 2.76 |
| blink_objects | 4.9 | 17.8 | +12.9 | 0.43 |
| gpu (transfer buffers, churn of 16 MB buffers) | 25.2 | 37.2 | +12.0 | 0.40 |
| partition_alloc | 218.9 | 227.6 | +8.7 | 0.29 |
| malloc | 31.4 | 37.6 | +6.3 | 0.21 |
| v8 | 11.8 | 12.3 | +0.5 | 0.02 |
| webgl | 18.6 | 18.5 | -0.1 | 0 |
| cc | 16.9 | 16.5 | -0.4 | 0 |

The growing category is Blink's own garbage-collected heap. Named objects in it: `blink::NetworkResourcesData::ResourceData` 3.6 to 14.0 MB (+10.4) and its hash table backing, which is the DevTools network agent's per-request record. Those named objects are about 12 MB of the 83 MB; the rest of `NormalPageSpace3` is heap pages whose contents the dump does not name, so I state that the growth is in the Blink GC heap and that the network agent record is **present and growing**, not that it accounts for all of it. `partition_alloc` is about 220 MB in every driver run (its `buffer` partition 215 MB) and about 10 to 25 MB without the driver (see 3), so the large steady block is also driver-linked.

### 2. What drives it (closed path, 620 s each, dumps at 200 and 610 s)
Renderer = OS sum of renderer processes. "Slope" is a least-squares fit after minute 6.7 (t > 400 s); "rule slope" is the ADR-0043 fit (after 180 s). Files: `motion_<kind>.json` (+ `.log`, `.dump*.json`), `dump_compare_motion_<kind>.md`.

| motion | what it does | renderer slope (t>400 s) | rule slope (t>180 s) | per-2-min medians (MB) | growing category (dump 200 to 610 s) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| still | no input, control | 0.13 | 0.24 | 118, 117, 123, 123, 123 | none (all categories +-0.1 MB; footprint 69.9 to 70.2) |
| wheel | real wheel turn every 2 s, no setView | -0.02 | 0.28 | 116, 114, 120, 120, 121 | none (footprint 64.1 to 65.2) |
| zoom | scripted width sweep, centre fixed | 1.75 | 2.59 | 252, 346, 356, 360, 366 | blink_gc +9.6 (1.39/min), partition_alloc +6.4, malloc +4.5 |
| pan | scripted centre sweep, width 1000 m | 1.62 | 2.66 | 196, 313, 345, 349, 352 | partition_alloc +10.4, blink_gc +9.0, malloc +6.8 |
| drag | real mouse drag every 2 s, no setView | 12.15 | 20.12 | 154, 202, 260, 303, 340, 342 | partition_alloc +114.1 (still filling, ends at 223 MB), blink_gc +5.4 |

- Still and wheel are flat and load no tiles beyond the first (shards 4 to 4; the wheel does not move the camera enough to load more). Zoom and pan, which keep requesting tiles, grow at about 1.6 to 1.8 MB/min by minute 7 and still climb; drag fills the same 220 MB `partition_alloc` block more slowly (a warm-up, not yet at a steady slope after 10 minutes), so a 10-minute run is too short for it. Layer toggles and day/night were not run: the categories do not point at the UI (nothing grows without tile traffic).
- In every run the driver-side block `partition_alloc` reaches about 220 to 230 MB once tiles flow, then `blink_gc` keeps rising.

Files: `nodriver_closed_20min.{json,log}`, `playwright_path_closed_20min.{json,log}`, `dump_compare_nodriver.md`, `dump_compare_playwright_path.md`. ## Deviations and assumptions
- Outcome (b) reached, so there was no fix and no 60-minute run; the one-hour no-driver run that would support ADR-0044 was **not** done (20 minutes only).
- Motion runs are 620 s, not the 10 to 15 minutes at the longer end; drag had not reached a steady slope.
- `--motion path` (scripted path, no real input) and `memory-nodriver.mjs` are additions beyond the card's wording, needed for the control.
- The dumps are taken after the sample of the interval and take a few seconds each; sampling is delayed slightly then. The tracing is on only during a dump.
- The page renderer is picked by having a `webgl` allocator; the session's OS sum also counts two small helper renderers. Windows `PrivateMemorySize64` and memory-infra `private_footprint_bytes` differ by definition, so only changes within one measure are compared.
- I did not isolate the exact Chrome component (whether DevTools `Network` agent buffering or another Playwright agent). Direct proof would be a Playwright session whose network agent is off, which the driver does not offer.
- 12 MB of raw dump summaries are committed (16 small JSON, about 0.65 MB each); raw traces (gzipped 0.8 MB each) are outside the repo.
- The vite build at the end emptied my `web/dist` (including the copied tiles); it is gitignored and unused after the runs.

## Open questions and risks
- **Decision for the orchestrator:** adopt ADR-0044 (renderer rule judged by `memory-nodriver.mjs`, driver-attached figure informational with a 250 MB/hour ceiling), or ask for a 60-minute no-driver run first. Until then `npm run memory` still fails the renderer rule on any run over about 8 minutes (3.3 to 3.9 MB/min against the 6 MB/min slope and the +20 % change).
- A real user without devtools should not see this growth, but I did not measure a user-like profile beyond the 20-minute no-driver run; a longer run would also cover the tile-eviction side.
- The same driver cost would apply to the e2e and bench runs (renderer memory only; fps not shown to be affected).
