## Deviations and assumptions
1. **Rotation buttons and day/night toggle stay top right** (the card allows this). The approved baselines show the toggle, so moving it would change them. At 412 px the toolbar zone is narrowed by 104 px (`calc(100vw - 12px - 104px)`) to stay clear of them; the search box fills row 1 and the Livelli and Mappa buttons sit on row 2. This fits, so the escalation condition did not trigger. The info card zone itself (T-5.1) is not placed by me: the toolbar uses the top-left only, and I have no way to test the overlap with T-5.1's card until the branches meet. The orchestrator should re-run the overlap check after merging T-5.1.
2. **Tiles notice moved** from `position:fixed` top left into the toolbar zone, below the toolbar (it would otherwise sit under the search box). Existing tests (`tiles`, `daynight`, `rotation`) still pass unchanged.
3. **Debug overlay moved** (`debug.ts`) from top left to the right, below the rotation buttons, because it covered the toolbar and broke `debug.spec.ts`. It is a dev-only overlay (`?debug`). At 412 px it can overlap the toolbar while popups are open.
4. **Results, layers panel and the 412 px minimap are in the normal flow of the toolbar stack** (no absolute popups). The tiles notice is therefore pushed down while they are open instead of being covered.
5. **Test state:** `toolbar.selection` exposes `currentSelection()` (for "check the selection state") and `toolbar.minimapView` is computed live from the camera, not from the last drawn frame. Key `toolbar` only; T-5.1 may add its own key.
6. **`?layers=` semantics (my choice):** comma list of the toggles that are ON, `none` for all off; empty or absent means no opinion. Toggle ids: `buildings, trees, markings, roads, waterGreen, landmarkDetails`. Remembered under localStorage `milano-iso.layers`, written only on a user toggle.
7. **`layerVisible(name)`** in `web/src/layers.ts` takes a tile layer (`buildings`, `road_areas`, `water`, ...) or a toggle id; unknown layers and `landmark` (a selection layer, no mesh) count as visible. T-5.1 should call it with the picked mesh layer.
8. **Fit rule:** `fitWidth` = max(box width across the screen, box height across the screen scaled by aspect) x 1.4, clamped to 60..4000 m. So a single small building zooms to the 60 m limit. The margin (1.4) and the 400 ms duration are style constants, not data. A street flies to its index `centre` (as the card says), not the bbox centre; a grouped hit flies to the union bbox centre.
9. **Choosing a hit with Enter and no active option takes the first hit** (my choice, convenient for keyboard users).
10. **Minimap on mobile** is fetched at start like on desktop (the card says nothing about lazy loading it). 412 px panel is 200 px wide, desktop 160 px.
11. **Visual baseline** `toolbar-*-win32.png` is skipped on non-win32 (card: win32 only). On the 1280x720 baseline the view polygon covers the whole fixture minimap, because the 600 m view is larger than the 482 m fixture map. That is correct, not a bug.
12. The preview server on 4183 for the real-data shots was started by me and stopped by PID after confirming only it was listening; the process command line could not be printed (wmic returned nothing).
13. I did not edit `docs/PROGRESS.md` or `docs/DECISIONS.md` (not listed in the card); layout decisions 1-4 above may deserve an ADR note.

## Amendment 1 - minimap night look (review finding)
- **Finding:** in `minimap-night-*.png` the minimap read as day colours.
- **Cause (checked, not guessed):** the theming plumbing was working. `data-mode` is set on `#minimap` from the first `setMode` (so it is correct for `?time=night` and stored night) and the night CSS beats the SVG's own `<style>` by id specificity. The original e2e already read the computed ground fill as 0x2e3650. What looked wrong was the colours: night buildings used the night palette `buildingOther` (0x858899), a light grey that dominates a dense minimap, and the night view polygon fill (alpha 0.2 yellow) tinted the whole 412 px minimap beige, because the footprint covers most of it there. So not one of the three suspects; a style problem plus weak tests (only ground, only one path).
- **Fix:** night minimap buildings are now 55% night `buildingOther` + 45% night ground (0x5e6378, `NIGHT_BUILDING_MIX` in `minimap.ts`); night view polygon fill alpha 0.07. These are style choices, not data.
- **Tests added** (`toolbar.spec.ts`, computed `fill` of `.mm-ground` and `.mm-building`): start with `?time=night`; day to night to day on desktop; at 412 px with the panel opened before the toggle, and opened after the toggle (including closing, toggling back to day, reopening). Unit tests for `mixRgb` and the building colour in `nightCss`.
- **Screenshots regenerated and viewed:** `reports/artifacts/T-5.3b/minimap-night-{1280x720,412x915}.png` (all other screenshots regenerated by the same script, content unchanged).
- **Verification:** typecheck and lint clean; unit 164 passed; full e2e 77 passed, three runs in a row (1.1m, 1.2m, 1.1m), default workers. Preview on 4183 for the screenshots was started by me and stopped by PID (the PID was the only listener on 4183).
