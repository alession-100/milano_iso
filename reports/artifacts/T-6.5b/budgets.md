# T-6.5b budgets on the city (VERIFICATION section 5), after the merged level-0 flat layers

This replaces the draw-call row of `reports/artifacts/T-6.5/budgets.md`. The draw-call budget is read as **settled views** (owner, 2026-11-22, ADR-0041): at most 300 calls once nothing is pending. Short peaks while a new level loads are recorded, not capped.

Machine: the same Windows 11 laptop as T-6.4 and T-6.5. Frame rate: Google Chrome (channel `chrome`), headed, real GPU as WebGL reports it: `ANGLE (Intel, Intel(R) Iris(R) Xe Graphics (0x0000A7A0) Direct3D11 vs_5_0 ps_5_0, D3D11)` (in every `bench_*.json`). Build: `vite build` of the T-6.5b head, served by `node web/scripts/serve.mjs --dir <dist> --port 4204`. Tileset: `tiles/city/` (copied from the T-6.5a data dir, files unchanged). Sweep, default view, notch, transit and timing: headless Chromium, software GL (draw calls, triangles and levels do not depend on the GPU).
