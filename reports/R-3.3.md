## Deviations and assumptions
- **Per-building colour uses one lookup material per (tile, mesh), not one shared material per layer.** three.js re-uploads material uniforms only when the material changes, so one shared Lambert material cannot switch its per-tile lookup texture between draws without hacks. All these materials have the same `customProgramCacheKey`, so there is one shader program and the draw-call count is unchanged (measured above). Flat layers (road_areas, rail, water, green, landuse) still share one material per layer. Lookup materials and textures are disposed with the tile.
- Only `buildings` and `roads` use the lookup (colour depends on feature fields); `road_areas` is one flat colour (its `class` values are not split by the spec).
- Edits outside the listed outputs, needed for the task: `scene.ts` (lights and colours moved to `style/`), `tiles/loader.ts` and its test import (feature table fetch, `applyStyle`), new `e2e/style.spec.ts`.
- The palette has 15 surface colours (limit 16). Light colours (`LIGHT_COLOURS`) are kept outside that palette. Ground and background share one colour.
- Building and road group mappings are my style categories; the OSM values they cover were checked against the pilot feature tables (e.g. `yes`, `apartments`, `office`, `church`, `commercial`, `public`, `kiosk`, `parking`, ...). Anything unlisted (including `yes`, `parking`, `roof`, `bridge`) is "other"/"minor". The fixture tables contain only `building_type = yes`; the unit test also checks known and unknown values explicitly.
- Landmarks (`landmark_id` not null) all get one accent colour; in the Duomo view several other red buildings appear, because 9 buildings in the pilot tiles carry a `landmark_id`.
- Sun direction (-0.35, 0.75, 0.70) normalised: +Z walls lit, +X walls fall back to the fill; shadows fall to the right on screen. Intensities (sun 2.6, fill 1.3), bias and normalBias are tuned by eye on the fixture and Duomo screenshots.
- The shadow frustum is fitted to the view footprint extended up by 150 m (style constant above the 100.2 m pilot maximum, ADR-0022) and clipped to the content bounds. The shadow map is re-rendered on every on-demand render (not only on camera change).
- The 4000 m view shows the 8 km ground plane as a hexagon (existing T-3.2 behaviour, not changed here).
- Frame-time numbers are headless software GL and only indicative; the ms measurement was taken by forcing re-renders with a 0.01 m zoom jitter.
- Source files are written with LF; git warns that CRLF working copies convert on touch (existing repo setting).
