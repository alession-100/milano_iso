## Deviations and assumptions
1. **Hausdorff metric.** `shapely.hausdorff_distance` between the decoded union and the source union gave up to 14 m on 45 groups, although the symmetric-difference area was about 1.8 m2. Cause: separately simplified volumes of one building leave small internal holes in the decoded union; hole vertices lie inside the source region but far from its boundary, and shapely measures to boundary vertices. The test therefore uses a region Hausdorff (`region_hausdorff`: boundary points densified at 0.25 m measured to the other polygon as a region, symmetric). Per-volume decoding is not possible because triangles are not tagged by volume; footprints are compared per (building, top height) group, source volumes with a top within half a quantisation unit of the group being unioned. The export-time per-polygon check (boundary semantics, <= 0.5 m) is unchanged. The decoded value includes the 2 cm quantisation.
2. Whether the internal sliver holes are visible is not assessed here (pre-existing behaviour of the export; the glb bytes must not change).
3. "Source feature inside the processed extent" = more than 0.01 m2 (areas) or 0.01 m (lines) inside the pilot AOI bbox, underground rows excluded as in the export.
4. Empty tiles: skipped; none exist in the pilot now (no 0-byte glb was present before), so this is covered by a unit test only.
5. `content_bbox_local` is rounded to 3 decimals; it is in the client frame (x, y, z) like the glb, not the [xmin, zmin, xmax, zmax] layout of `bbox_local`.
6. `tests/data/tile_decode.py` and `test_data_tile_content.py` are new files in the permitted `tests/data/*`. `docs/PROGRESS.md` was not edited (not in the card's file list).
