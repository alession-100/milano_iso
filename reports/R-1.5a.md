## Deviations and assumptions
- The relation does not carry the QID; the link is geometric only (as the card's rule intends).
- Overpass `timestamp_areas_base` for the is_in query was 2026-11-15T13:22:36Z, a few hours older than osm_base; is_in uses precomputed areas.
- I ran three fetch commands concurrently once (Wikidata, OSM API, Overpass: different hosts), so the 1 req/s limit was held per host, not strictly globally.
- `ruff format` was needed after my edits; no behaviour change.
- The centroid is 52.56 m from Wikidata P625, farther than the node was; the building is a large complex, so a mismatch beyond the 5 m tolerance is expected. Tolerance semantics for later phases are the orchestrator's call.
