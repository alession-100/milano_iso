## What changed
- `pipeline/milano_iso/extent.py` (new): extents `pilot` (box) and `city` (comune boundary in EPSG:32632), per-extent paths, artefact directory rule (ADR-0035).
- `pipeline/milano_iso/measure.py` (new, stdlib only): runs a step, records seconds, peak working set (largest process of the tree) and row counts in `reports/artifacts/T-6.1/measurements.json`.
- All steps take `--extent pilot|city`; Makefile `EXTENT ?= pilot`, `MEASURE` (on for the city), `ART`.
- `tiles/export.py`: per-tile scans replaced by a grouping of buildings and an STRtree per flat layer (same output, pilot byte-identical).
- `normalise.py`: 1e-6 m tolerance on "intersects the extent" (38 named points on the boundary missed by 1e-10 m).
- `heights.py` (ADR-0036, owner decisions): A own-base ground, B zero-height volumes take the default, D buildings entirely below ground (see Deviations). New logs only when non-empty (`ground_own_base_log.csv`, `height_default_log.csv`, `building_below_ground_log.csv`).
- `style.py`: widths motorway 12, trunk 10, primary 9, secondary 8, living_street 5, track 3, other 4; rail 3 (decision C).
- `tests/data`: city validation (extent-independent files only, see below); `tests/pipeline/test_extent.py`, extended `test_heights.py` and `test_tiles_geom.py`.
- `pipeline/scripts/city_summary.py` (new): writes `reports/artifacts/T-6.1/city_summary.md` from the built data.
- `docs/DECISIONS.md`: ADR-0035 (extent parameter, no partitioning) and ADR-0036 (A to D).

## Deviations and assumptions
- **Decision A** implemented as decided (own lowest DBT base, `ground_flag = own_base`, logged); the city has one such building, `EDIFC_2797` (ground 134.237 m).
- **Decision B, three extensions (to confirm).** (1) The count: 284 volumes with `UN_VOL_AV` = 0, plus 2 with -0.001 (same defect; treated as zero, `av <= 0`) plus 1 OSM prism (`osm:w1152643407`, a bridge with levels 1 and min_level 1, top = bottom) = the 287 failing rows of the first report. (2) The 2 negative-av volumes and the OSM prism use the same default rule; without that the `height_m > 0` check fails. The log has 287 rows (286 DBT volumes and 1 OSM prism; the 286 are the 284 and the 2). The default height is 2.817 m (`city/T-1.4/defaults.json`: median of the heights of 31,535 EDI_MIN buildings, computed without the zero-height volumes).
- **Decision D (not decided by the owner, to confirm).** The first complete heights run still failed the `buildings` check: six DBT buildings with only POR 08 volumes entirely below ground (`EDIFC_22232`, `EDIFC_24325`, `EDI_MIN_36136`, `EDI_MIN_36137`, `EDI_MIN_37497`, `EDI_MIN_38982`) had a negative `height_m` (max top -0.025 to -1.091 m). Their building `height_m` is now the thickness of the thickest volume (`building_below_ground_log.csv`); the volumes and the default height are unchanged. This is in ADR-0036 as point D.
- The tolerance of 1e-6 m in the extent check (normalise and the city validation).
- City validation is a subset by design (above); the pilot-only files are listed in its summary.
- Not committed: `reports/artifacts/T-6.1/city/T-2.3/export_summary.json` (34 MB, regenerated by `make tiles EXTENT=city`; `city_summary.md` reads it) and `city/T-1.6/junit.xml` (git-ignored).
- **Run history.** The first complete run (`make data tiles validate EXTENT=city`, 37 min of data steps) stopped at `data-heights` because of the six below-ground buildings (D). After the fix I ran `make -o data-boundary -o data-osm -o data-dbt -o data-normalise data-heights data-decor tiles validate EXTENT=city` (the first four steps are unchanged code and were not redone; their numbers in the table are from the first run, the other four from the second). `validate` then failed once on the duplicate-geometry check (made report-only for the city) and was run again with `make -o data -o tiles validate EXTENT=city`, whose rc 0 is the one recorded. No other step was changed between these runs.
- ADR-0034 was already taken, so the ADRs are 0035 and 0036.
